# NRI Creative Analysis — Executive Summary

**Date:** 2026-05-06 · **Owner:** Godfather · **Status:** v6 FINAL shipped — playbook-aligned

## What we asked

What creative content moves the needle for the NRI segment? CPTD < ₹35K across markets. Hooks, colours, video length, badges. Specifically: does Top 1% Tutors badge change CPTD?

## What we learned (after 5 rounds of correction)

### True Meta-only NRI CPTD is ₹40-55K range — above the ₹35K threshold

The brand playbook isn't an optimization exercise. It's a threshold-failure intervention.

| Market | True Meta NRI CPTD (mature window) |
|---|---|
| US | ₹40-50K |
| ROW (APAC + UK) | ₹40-55K |
| India | ₹40-50K (channel-level failure — separate problem) |

### What matters (Meta-only, validated)

| Lever | Lift | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Trustpilot ★★★★★ overlay | -24% CPTD | High |
| MathFit Application dimension named | -23% vs Unclear | High |
| LAL_PayU_IndianAud × Static (workhorse) | -51% vs LAL_Enrolled × Static | High |
| Cuemath named in copy | -8% US, -18% ROW | Medium (validate w/ A/B) |
| Holiday-coded creative | -16% vs evergreen US | Medium |

### What doesn't matter (data-falsified)

- **Specificity (Anonymous vs Named-parent vs Named-child)** — all within ₹3K, format-driven
- **Static vs Video for US NRI** — essentially tied (8% diff, was 27% claim)
- **"Top 1% Tutors" badge alone** — slightly worse than no badge

### What we couldn't test — the brand moat itself

| Playbook claim | Reality |
|---|---|
| Coach visible via Tenure or Memory framing → cheaper CPTD | <10 of 1,300 ads carry it. We don't make the brand we claim. |
| Three-beat shape (Goal → Mechanism → Proof) → cheaper | Not coded in tagger. Untestable. |

The biggest finding: **the brand playbook is directionally validated where testable, but the corpus barely contains the brand-defining signals.** Production gap, not data gap.
