Common questions from premium-curriculum parents

What parents ask before booking.

Why does Cuemath cost more than other tutors I've spoken to?
Because we are built differently. Specialist tutors from the top 1% of applicants, 1:1 every class, the same teacher from trial onward, a written plan, daily progress notes, and a monthly plan you can leave any time. If a tutor costs less, one of those line items is missing. That is the trade-off.
What does the price include?
A 60-minute 1:1 live class with your dedicated tutor, lesson planning aligned to your child's curriculum, daily progress notes after each class, and a WhatsApp summary post-trial that you can share at home. No add-on fees, no platform charges.
Is the trial really free?
Yes. The first 1:1 class is free. No credit card. After the trial we send you a written plan and the price. You decide.
Will the trial teacher be the same one teaching ongoing classes?
Yes. Continuity is non-negotiable. If the fit isn't right after the trial, we match you with another specialist for a second free trial — at no charge.
How will I know my child is actually progressing?
Daily progress notes after each class. If we spot a gap or risk early, we flag it — we don't wait three months for it to show up in a school report.
Do you actually teach the IB and IGCSE curriculum, or just generic math?
Our tutors are trained on the boards they teach — IB (PYP / MYP / DP), IGCSE / Cambridge, and the American and British international tracks. In the trial, the tutor will show you exactly which units they would work on for your child's grade.
I want to discuss with my partner before deciding.
Of course. After the trial, we send a WhatsApp summary covering what we found, what we'd work on, and the price — easy to share at home and decide together.
Do you teach CBSE and ICSE too?
Yes — but this page is for families on IB, IGCSE and international curricula, where the program is priced for that specialisation. If you are on CBSE or ICSE, we have a separate plan; mention it when you book and the team will route you correctly.
