Common questions from IB parents

What parents ask before booking.

Do you actually teach the IB curriculum, or just generic math?
Our tutors are IB-trained and lesson plans map directly to PYP, MYP, and DP frameworks. In the trial, we'll show you exactly which units we'd work on for your child's grade.
Will the trial teacher be the same one teaching ongoing classes?
Yes. Continuity is non-negotiable for us. If for any reason the fit isn't right after the trial, we'll match you with a senior IB tutor for a second free trial — no charge.
How will I know my child is actually progressing?
You'll receive a written plan after the trial and monthly progress notes. If we spot a gap or risk early, we flag it — we don't wait for a parent–teacher conference three months later.
What does it cost?
Starting from 60 AED/class on the monthly plan. No annual lock-in. Sibling discount if you enrol more than one child.
I want to discuss with my partner before deciding.
Of course. After your free trial, we'll send a WhatsApp summary covering what we found, what we'd work on, and pricing — easy to share at home.
My child is doing fine — is this for us?
Yes. About 1 in 4 of our IB families come to us for enrichment or Olympiad prep, not remediation. We tailor the path to where your child is.
