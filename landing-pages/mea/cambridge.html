Common questions from Cambridge parents

What parents ask before booking.

Are you actually aligned with Cambridge or just generic math?
We teach to the Cambridge framework — Primary, Lower Secondary, and IGCSE. In the trial, we'll show you which units we'd work on for your child's stage.
I have more than one child. Do you handle that?
Yes — and well. Many of our families have two or three children at different stages. Each child gets their own plan and tutor, with a sibling discount on the second and third.
My child is in IGCSE Year 10/11. Do you do exam prep?
Yes — Cambridge IGCSE Math (0580 and 0606), past papers, mark schemes, exam technique. We don't shortcut the concepts though — that's where most prep tutors fall down.
Will the trial teacher continue afterwards?
Yes. Continuity is the rule. If the fit isn't right after the trial, we'll match you with a more senior tutor for a second free trial — no charge.
What does it cost?
Starting from 60 AED/class on the monthly plan. No annual lock-in. Sibling discount on every additional child.
I want to discuss with my partner before deciding.
Of course. After your trial, we'll send a WhatsApp summary covering what we found, what we'd work on, and pricing — easy to share at home.
