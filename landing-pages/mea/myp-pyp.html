Common questions from PYP & MYP parents

What parents ask before booking.

My child is in Grade 2 — is online really right at this age?
For 1:1 sessions, yes. The screen is just the medium — the relationship with the teacher is what matters, and that builds the same way it does in person. Most of our PYP families say the kids look forward to class.
My child enjoys math. Why would we need a tutor?
Many of our PYP and MYP families come for enrichment, not remediation. We have an Olympiad track and "go beyond school" path for kids who already love math and want more.
How will the trial actually work?
45 minutes, 1:1, with an IB-trained tutor. By the end you'll have a written summary of what your child knows and where the gaps are. No pressure to continue.
Will it be the same teacher every time?
Yes. Continuity is the rule. If the fit isn't right after the trial, we'll match you with a more senior tutor for a second free trial — no charge.
What does it cost?
Starting from 60 AED/class on the monthly plan. No annual lock-in. Sibling discount if you enrol more than one child.
I want to discuss with my partner before deciding.
Of course. After your trial, we'll send a WhatsApp summary covering what we found, what we'd work on, and pricing — easy to share at home.
