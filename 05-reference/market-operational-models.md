# Market operational truth — per market, never borrow across

A claim verified for India does **not** automatically apply to APAC. A claim from APAC does not transfer to UK. Each market has its own commercial + operational reality. Always verify per-market before writing copy.

Last updated: 2026-05-12 (late evening) — verified pricing tables added for all 5 markets after direct cuemath.com website crawl; tenure-based plan structure confirmed universal (India included); class frequency K-8 = 2/wk · HS = 3/wk added; US pricing-page bug flagged (shows INR not USD); website-vs-brand-lock gaps logged without changing locks.

**Earlier May 12 updates:** class duration unified, tutor/coach split locked, academic counsellor re-confirmed global, UK exam-prep expanded, "fee" + "tenure" customer-facing locks added.

## Banned cross-market borrowing

- **Don't** put "monthly plan" / "no annual lock-in" on APAC or UK LPs. They use tenure.
- **Don't** put "Admissions Manager" in parent-facing copy in any market. Use "academic counsellor."
- **Don't** put "teacher" in any parent-facing copy in any market. Use coach (brand) or tutor (performance).
- **Don't** put NCEA / PSLE packaged-prep claims anywhere. They don't exist as packaged products.
- **Don't** put NAPLAN / OC / Selective / 11+ outside the relevant market. They're geo-specific.
- **Don't** put ₹ pricing, Bangalore references, Hindi VO, Indian tutor visuals on ANZ / UK / MEA non-Indian creative. NRI US is the exception (and even there, the Indian-method positioning is currently unresolved between Apr 25 founder DBS and May 12 global-pivot feedback — see open Naina decision).
- **Don't** quote specific discount percentages yourself in any market. Defer to academic counsellor.

## How to apply

When writing any LP / ad / email / trial script:
1. **Identify the market first.**
2. **Pull the right reference**: India = India LP + Naina sign-off; APAC/UK = Trial Mastery doc + this file; ROW gaps = ask Naina or skip the claim.
3. **Apply the cross-market constants** (55 min, fee, tenure, academic counsellor, coach/tutor split, no teacher).
4. **When market-specific fact is unknown**: defer to the academic counsellor / trial conversation rather than invent.
