## 1. Positioning

- **Mission:** the most trusted math brand in the world — the go-to place for everything math, through a coach who stays for years.
- **Core promise:** *We make every child MathFit™ — through a coach who stays for years.*
- **The two pillars:** (1) every child has genuine potential — anti-elitist. (2) Coaches own outcomes, not just explain concepts.
- **Six beliefs:** anxiety is about teaching method, not math · productive struggle is the method · MathFit is built, not innate · thinking > memorising in the AI era · the 1:1 coach is irreplaceable · cuing, not telling.

### Tagline tiers

| Tier | Line | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Signature | *Making Kids MathFit™* | Default. Ships on the logo lockup |
| 2 — Sentence | *We make every child MathFit™.* | Headlines, body copy |
| 3 — Full | *We make every child MathFit™ — through a coach who stays for years.* | Overview films, investor decks |

**™ rule (house rule, retained from v1):** ™ on first prominent mention, headlines, hero sections, end frames, section headers. May be dropped in running body copy after first use. Never `MathFit` / `mathfit` / `Math Fit`.

### Logo on creative

| Surface | Lockup |
|---|---|
| Performance creative | Wordmark only, no tagline — cleaner, higher CTR |
| Brand surface | Wordmark + signature tagline |
| Long-form | Wordmark + sentence form |

Never mix two logo versions on one screen.

## 2. Voice

**Archetype: The Coach.** Trusted, human, wise, empowering.

Traits: authoritative without condescension · direct and precise · energising and forward-moving · honest, never overselling · curious — asks questions, invites thinking.

| Audience | Register |
|---|---|
| Parents | Aspirational + confident. **Never shame-coded or fear-based** |
| Children | Warm + respectful. Never patronising |
| Coaches | Collegial + direct |

### The five sensory translations

1. **Considered Calm** — trust before a word is spoken. Generous space, one idea per section, 70%+ neutral ground, gold as punctuation.
2. **Precise Warmth** — care shown through exactness. Warm neutrals, exact alignment, confident weights.
3. **Revealed Clarity** — structure shown, not hidden. Visible hierarchy, visible grid, geometric construction.
4. **Deliberate Restraint** — holding back as generosity. Fewer CTAs, one primary action, minimal explanation.
5. **Hidden Logic** — the system felt before it's found. Ratio-driven layout, craft in details, asymmetric balance.

## 3. Visual identity — Sunlit Gold

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Sunlit Gold (`gold-500`) | `#F4AB52` | **Accent and CTA only.** Never a flood |
| Warm Charcoal (`neutral-1000`) | `#292827` | Text, headings, dark surfaces |
| Warm Off-White (`neutral-100`) | `#FAF8F5` | Page ground, light surfaces |

Seven full ramps exist as design tokens: Warm Neutral (10 steps), Gold (11), Success, Warning, Error, Info, Purple (11 each). Consume tokens, not raw hex, wherever a token system is available.

**Excluded, absolutely:** pure black · pure white · cool greys · red-accent anxiety signals.

**Ratio:** 70%+ neutral ground. Gold is punctuation.

**Typography:** Athletics (substance — headings, weight) · Untitled Sans (infrastructure — UI, body).

**Illustration:** fill `300` / shadow `400` / stroke `500` from one colour family, 2 colours ideal and 3 max · built only from grids, circles, rectangles, cubes — nothing freehand · pick 2D flat OR isometric, never mix in one illustration · all lines 1px, boundaries solid, structural lines dashed (dash 1 · gap 1).

> **Retired:** Navy `#1A1A2E` and yellow `#F5A623` are dead. Any asset, agent rule, or dashboard style still carrying them is out of date.

## 4. Photography, casting, and imagery

### Light signature (non-negotiable)

Warm, soft, directional morning window light. **4800–5200K · gentle 2:1 contrast · 45° from upper-left.**

### The four dimensions

1. **Light signature** — as above.
2. **Hidden logic layer** — mathematical geometry quietly embedded: bookshelf rhythm, proportional spacing, structural parallels.
3. **Emotional register** — calm engagement. Capture **the moment before understanding.** Never staged smiles.
4. **Environment as character** — warm, lived-in space: wood, paper, brass, books. Never institutional classrooms.

### Casting

| Category | Rule |
|---|---|
| Default mix | **40% South Asian / NRI · 40% other Asian · 20% non-Asian** |
| K–5 creative | All women coaches |
| Middle + HS | 50% women, 50% men |
| HS specifically | Slight male lean (perceived credibility for advanced math) |
| Coach age on camera | Late 30s default — expert-reading, present-appearing |
| Children | Age-appropriate to grade tier. **Faces may be shown** |
| Single-face targeting | Lead face matches the targeted ethnicity; supporting cast always includes one non-Asian face for the global-premium signal |

### Coach on camera

- **Attire:** smart casuals, global professional. Crisp shirt or blouse, clean blazer optional, solid colours, warm neutrals or sunlit-gold accent. **Never saris, suits, kurtas, or Indian-wear.**
- **Setting:** warm home office, soft daylight. Light wood desk, books visible, a plant, natural window light, neutral wall.
- **Expression:** confident, warm, attentive. Direct eye contact, slight smile — not big. Engaged, leaning slightly toward camera, composed posture.
- **Direction:** show the coach **listening more than speaking** · show hand-writing on screen (real pen, real problem) · **never show coach and child in the same physical room** — the split-screen is the online signature.

### Failure modes

- **The AI look** — uncanny lighting, over-smooth skin, plastic surfaces.
- **The stock-photo look** — staged smiles, flat institutional light, generic environments.

### Layout DO / DON'T

**DO:** generous white space · 70%+ neutral, gold as punctuation · warm palette throughout · asymmetric but balanced, ratio-driven (2:3, 3:5, golden) · one primary action per screen · mathematical structure selectively visible (partial grids, guide circles) · real faces, real outcomes, real durations · slow, deliberate motion.

**DON'T:** visual noise or competing CTAs · density that feels like shouting · pure black, pure white, cool greys · red-accent fear signals (countdowns, urgency) · cartoon characters or infantilising imagery · institutional or clinical coldness · luxury-brand distancing · dashboard-as-hero · staged smiles or stock-photo energy · two logo versions on one screen.

## 5. Creative structure — the Brand Beats

Renamed here to **Brand Beats (B1 / B2 / B3)** to stop the collision with the NRI *narrative* beats (situation → shift → proof). See conflict ledger §9.C.

| Beat | What it is | Leads on |
|---|---|---|
| **B1 — Goal** | The parent's transactional goal: grade improvement, acceleration, exam prep, competition. Must be **specific** | Performance creative |
| **B2 — Mechanism** | The coach relationship + curriculum + platform. Same coach for years, tier-tuned to grade | Brand creative |
| **B3 — MathFit** | The approach (productive struggle, cuing-not-telling) + the destination (the MathFit kid) | Brand creative |

**Asset rule:** performance = B1 primary + B2 **or** B3. Brand = B2 + B3, skip B1. Every asset carries at least one beat, a visible wordmark, and at least one MathFit reference.

### Two opening rules

- **Static headline test:** if the parent has to read past the headline to know what we're saying, the static failed. Lead with the most specific, most surprising line you have.
- **Video first 3 seconds:** no logos, no titles, no "this is an ad." Open on a human moment, a real number, or a contradiction.

## 6. The six USPs (verbatim)

1. **Top 1% global tutors** — coaches who specialise deeply in your child's thinking. *Proof: 4,000 tutors · top 1% selected · STEM background · trained on the MathFit method.*
2. **1:1 personalisation** — the fastest way to build real mathematical confidence: pace, depth and direction matched to your child, not the class average.
3. **Deep conceptual learning** — understanding that lasts even when formulas are forgotten. The why beneath the what.
4. **Platform & gamification** — engagement that fuels thinking, not distraction. A platform built for math practice, not for screen time.
5. **One program · three tracks** — one program for school curriculum, competitions and enrichment courses. The same coach, the same method, flexed to whichever track the child needs that year.
6. **Parent experience** — transparency, flexibility, zero fear of commitment. The way a parent wants to be treated by a long-term partner.

> USP 1 does **not** unban the `Top 1%` shorthand. [[forbidden-patterns]] §1.4 still governs: usable when the asset's job is to explain coach selection **and** it is paired with a mechanism or a what-this-means-for-your-kid line. Never as a bare premium badge.

## 7. Trust numbers (brand-book figures — see gate below)

| Metric | Figure |
|---|---|
| Happy parents | **400,000+** across **80+** countries |
| Parent improvement reporting | **97.2%** say their child improved |
| Competition wins | **2,500+** (AMC 8, MATHCOUNTS, Olympiad) |
| Trustpilot | **#1 Tutoring service** · ★★★★★ · **10K+** reviews |
| Refund rate | **<1%** |
| Tutors in network | **4,000** |
| Tutor selectivity | Top 1% of applicants |

**Usage rule (brand book):** pair one number with a specific claim — never bare. Example: *"97.2% of parents say their child improved. Three years with the same coach is how."*

### ⚠️ The paid-copy gate

Only these are cleared for **paid** US copy today, per the locked verified-facts table: `200,000+ students` · `400,000 parents` · `4.9★` · `From $20/class` · `20% off annual plans` · `Aligned with US Common Core` · `Built by MIT & Stanford alumni` (broad/conquest) / `Built by IIT & Stanford alumni` (NRI).

`97.2%` · `2,500+` · `<1%` · `4,000 tutors` · `80+ countries` are **new to the Brain** and are **not yet in a verified-facts table.** They are usable on **organic** brand surfaces (channel copy, films, playlist descriptions) and blocked in paid until Naina confirms the source. See ledger §9.A.

`#1 Tutoring service` carries a separate, non-brand problem: Google Ads disapproves unsubstantiated `#1` / `Best` superlatives at review. Do not put it in Google Ads text assets regardless of brand approval.

## 8. Vocabulary

### Own — use consistently

**MathFit™** (capitalised, one word) · **coach / coaching** (preferred over tutor — see surface rule below) · **program / 1:1 program** (implies curriculum + coach + measurement) · **mathematical thinking** (not "math skills") · **problem solvers · thinkers** (never "memorizers") · **understand · reason · explain** (core verbs) · **"She now [explains / attempts / tries]…"** — observed behaviour, not adjectives.

### Use with care

| Word | Condition |
|---|---|
| Tutor / tutoring | Only where a search keyword demands the lexical match — perf SEM, and YouTube search-facing metadata. See surface rule |
| Struggling | Only when framed positive |
| Confidence | Always paired with something earned |
| Foundation gap | Frame as fixable, never as verdict |
| Trusted / personalised / proven / world-class | Only with a specific anchor attached |
| Behind / ahead of school | Only with a specific grade-level reference |
| Fun | Junior only (K–2). **Never HS** |

### Never use

Competitor names (implicit contrast is fine) · **remedial · catch-up · speed math · quick fix** · generic edtech (**gamified · AI-powered learning journey · holistic**) · **crush math** (always) · *master math* and *ace the test* only ever paired with pedagogy · **limited time · only 3 seats left** and every other manufactured urgency · **genius · prodigy · gifted-only** (excludes the buyer) · **AI-powered as positioning** — it's a tool, not the product.

### Internal-only — never in external copy

**Productive struggle** → translate to observable behaviour. **Cuing, not telling** → translate to *"the coach asks the next question."* **FUAR · interleaving · retrieval practice** → never in parent-facing copy of any kind.

> This is wider than the old rule. [[forbidden-patterns]] §3.3 banned framework terms in parent **VO**; v2 bans them in **all** external copy — on-screen text and end cards included. Frameworks are how we brief, not how we speak.

### Coach vs tutor — surface rule (retained, extended)

| Surface | Word |
|---|---|
| Brand surfaces — films, OOH, hub films, thumbnail copy, playlist titles, channel copy | **Coach** |
| Performance surfaces — Google Ads, Meta, brand-keyword SEM | **Tutor** (lexical match earns Quality Score and matches the parent's search language) |
| YouTube search-facing metadata — video titles, tags, description keyword lines | **Tutor** permitted for lexical match |
| Anywhere, parent-facing | **Never "teacher"** |

## 9. Conflict ledger — open items

Each item: what collides, how it's resolved in the Brain today, and whether Naina still needs to rule.

### A. New trust numbers are unverified — ⚠️ NEEDS NAINA

`97.2%` · `2,500+ competition wins` · `<1% refund` · `4,000 tutors` · `80+ countries`. In the brand book, absent from every verified-facts table. **Resolved as:** organic-only, blocked in paid. **Needs:** confirmation of source so they can enter the US verified-facts table.

### B. Children's faces — RESOLVED BY SCOPE

Brand book: *"faces may be shown."* Forge Hard Rule 19: *"never generate images of identifiable children."* Not actually a conflict once scoped: brand book governs **photography** with real, released talent; Forge's rule governs **AI generation** — and the brand book independently bans "the AI look." **Resolved as:** real photography may show children's faces (with releases). AI-generated children stay banned. Forge rule 19 rescoped.

### C. Two frameworks both called "three beats" — RESOLVED BY RENAME

Brand book: Goal / Mechanism / MathFit. NRI creative review `three_beat_compliance`: situation → shift → proof. **Resolved as:** brand book's are **Brand Beats (B1/B2/B3)**; the NRI ones are **Narrative Beats**. Never write "three-beat" unqualified again.

### D. "Cue, don't tell" — RESOLVED, FORGE RULE CORRECTED

Forge Hard Rule 18 required referencing "Cue, don't tell" when writing about method. Brand book classes it internal-only. **Resolved as:** reference the *behaviour* — "the coach asks the next question," "understanding before answers" — never the term. Forge rule 18 rewritten.

### E. Colour system — RESOLVED, PROPAGATED

Navy `#1A1A2E` + yellow `#F5A623` retired for Sunlit Gold `#F4AB52` / Warm Charcoal `#292827` / Warm Off-White `#FAF8F5`. Propagated into Forge. **Still carrying old values:** `index.html` and the dashboard's own styling — cosmetic, unaudited, out of scope until someone chooses to reskin.

### F. Coach and child never in the same room — NEW RULE, ADOPTED

No prior Cuemath doc carried this. It constrains any composition showing coach + child together — they must be split-screen, screen-in-frame, or over-the-shoulder-at-a-laptop. **Directly governs the YouTube thumbnail rebuild.**

### G. Attire and casting — NEW RULES, ADOPTED

No saris / suits / kurtas / Indian-wear on coaches. 40/40/20 casting mix. Grade-tier gender rules. Coach late-30s. Added to [[forbidden-patterns]] §1.5. This is the same brand logic as the `Indian Tutors` hard ban, extended to wardrobe.

### H. "Top 1%" — RECONCILED, NO CHANGE

Brand book gives it as USP 1 with proof attached; [[forbidden-patterns]] §1.4 permits it in coach-selection contexts paired with a mechanism. The two agree. The ban on the bare premium badge stands.

### I. Superseded v1 content — PARTIALLY RETAINED

`brand-guidelines.md` v1 keeps two things v2 does not restate: the **Copy Atoms** list and the **Transformation Narrative** (before → after). Both remain usable. Its 4 USPs, 6 pillars, colour values, and messaging hierarchy are superseded.

### J. Coach vs tutor on YouTube — ⚠️ NEEDS NAINA

Extending the surface rule to YouTube splits one channel across two words: `tutor` in titles (search), `coach` on screen and in thumbnail / playlist copy (brand). Defensible — YouTube search behaves like Google, YouTube watching behaves like film. **Needs:** sign-off before it's treated as locked.
