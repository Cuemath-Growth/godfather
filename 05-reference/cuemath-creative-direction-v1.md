Three lines. Tape them above your monitor.

## 1 · Why this document exists

We've been saying too many things at once. Free trials, NRI rigour, top tutors, AMC prep, summer slide, ₹X/class, 30-day plans, "world-class," "personalized," "loved by parents." Each true, each meaningful, each interchangeable with a competitor's claim.

The result: the brand image fragments. Parents remember a feature. They don't remember **us**. The narrative leaks across 300 ads instead of compounding into one shape.

This document collapses every Cuemath communication into a single shape, so 300 production ads become one company's voice — and the brand compounds across every asset instead of fragmenting into them.

The promise: a junior copywriter, a senior designer, an external agency, and the founder, all working from this doc, will produce work that looks like it came from the same brand.

## 3 · What MathFit IS — the destination, defined

MathFit is **mathematical thinking expressed as a built skill**. Like physical fitness — you can't outsource it, you can't drill it, you can't download it. You build it, week by week, with the right coach, until it becomes part of how the child sees the world.

MathFit is the durable layer **underneath** grades, scores, and competitions. AI made calculation free. MathFit is what calculation cannot do.

Three dimensions. Named in language a parent already uses. Memorise these.

| Dimension | What it is | What a parent sees in their kitchen |
|---|---|---|
| **Clarity** | Understands the WHY before the HOW | She **explains her steps** before she solves. |
| **Application** | Solves problems she has never seen before | She **attempts unfamiliar problems** instead of freezing. |
| **Confidence** | Stays engaged when math gets hard | She **tries, fails, retries** — instead of asking for the answer. |

### The discipline

**Every Cuemath asset names exactly ONE of the three dimensions.** Pick the one the parent's goal points to. Don't list all three. Don't blur them. Don't bury them.

### The mapping (memorise this too)

| Parent goal | Dimension to lead with |
|---|---|
| Grade improvement | **Clarity** |
| Acceleration / advanced placement | **Application** |
| Conceptual depth / "she gets it but…" | **Clarity** |
| Competition (AMC, Math Kangaroo, MATHCOUNTS, Olympiad) | **Confidence** |
| SAT / ACT / standardised | **Confidence** |
| Foundation / early grades | **Clarity** |
| Summer slide / continuity | **Clarity** |
| Honors track / placement | **Application** |

If you cannot name the dimension before you start writing, you do not have a brief yet.

## 4 · How MathFit gets built — the mechanism

**One coach. Same child. For years.**

That's the entire how. Everything else is supporting evidence.

The coach is not a tutor. She is the long-term mentor who watches your child grow up. She remembers what your daughter was stuck on last week. She picks up in November where she left off in May. She is your child's coach in 2nd grade and your child's coach in 5th. The relationship is the unit. The duration is the moat.

Every other delivery model in the category — apps, group classes, marketplaces, AI tutors, neighbourhood centres — resets, rotates, or refreshes. We don't. That is the only thing no one else can claim, and it is the only thing we will claim relentlessly.

### The supporting USPs — ranked. Use exactly one per asset, never a list.

| # | USP | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Same coach for years** (85% Y1 retention; 60.8% of positive reviews name the coach) | Default. Every asset, unless the asset specifically requires a different supporting USP. |
| 2 | **1-in-200 hire rate** (proof of WHO that coach is) | When credibility is the parent's primary objection. HS, NRI, premium positioning. |
| 3 | **1:1 every session, never group** (proof of HOW the time is spent) | When the alternative the parent is comparing to is group-class (Mathnasium, Kumon, school enrichment). |
| 4 | **Indian-trained pedagogy** (proof of the depth tradition) | NRI segment only. Never on non-NRI US, ANZ, UK, MEA. |
| 5 | **Manan + named tutor credentials + outcome data** | 6–12 only. The expertise register. Not for K–5. |

### The coach must be visible

In every asset, in some form:
- **Named** on screen (Coach Aditi · Coach Meghana · Coach Karthik)
- **Or** by tenure ("Year 2 with Coach Aditi" · "Same coach since first grade")
- **Or** by the specific thing she remembers about the child ("She remembers what he was stuck on last week.")

A claim about "a tutor" is not the coach. A claim about "1:1" is not the coach. A claim about "personalized" is not the coach. **The coach has a name, a face, and a memory of your child.**

## 5 · The three-beat shape — every comm, no exceptions

Every static, every reel, every LP, every email, every organic post follows the same three beats. Three beats, in this order, every time:

| Beat | Job | The Cuemath shape |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Goal** | Names the outcome the parent walked in asking for | "B− isn't a math problem." |
| **2 · Mechanism** | Explains the goal through ONE MathFit dimension | "It's a thinking problem. We teach her to explain her steps before she solves." |
| **3 · Destination** | Names what we're building, attributed to long-term mentorship | "Building MathFit Clarity. One coach. Class by class." |

CTA: **always "Book a free 1:1 class."** US data: Free-Class close earns ₹13K CPTD vs Offer-led ₹43K — a 3× efficiency gap. Discount-led closes are not a creative choice; they are a margin leak with no upside.

(Exception: HS SAT/AP gets "Free SAT diagnostic" or "Free strategy call." Same logic — no offer, just the next 1:1.)

## 6 · The closing card — sacred, repeated, never improvised

Every Cuemath asset ends on the same close. The repetition is the point: 300 creatives, one closing shape, one company's voice. This is what makes a campaign a brand.

**Format:** dark card · centred · serif headline (Fraunces) · sans body (Inter) · italic emphasis on *MathFit*.

**One approved close. Verbatim, every asset. Statics, reels, LPs, emails, influencer, organic — same close.**

> Same Cuemath Coach.
> Every Single Class.
> Making Your Child *MathFit*.

CTA sits above or beside the close, never inside it: **"Book a free 1:1 class."** (HS exception per §5: "Free SAT diagnostic" / "Free strategy call.")

> The close keeps the arrow true. If 30 reference ads land on the same closing card, the brand compounds. If 30 land on 30 different closes, we ran 30 ads for nothing.

## 7 · Universal rules

### Always do

- **Lead with the parent's goal.** Grades, acceleration, SAT, AMC, fundamentals. Goal first, in the parent's language.
- **Name exactly one MathFit dimension.** Clarity, Application, or Confidence — not all three, not "math thinking" generically.
- **Make the long-term coach visible.** Named, on screen, with the same child for years.
- **Close on the fletch.** Pick the right length variant. Do not improvise the close.
- **Write the way a mother would text her sister tomorrow.** If the line wouldn't survive that test, it doesn't survive ours.
- **Use "your child's"** (possessive). Every competitor says "your child." We say "your child's coach." Different verb of ownership.

### Never do

- **No bleached language.** Strike-list: *personalized · trusted · world-class · best · leading · proven · top-rated · #1 · expert · premier · industry-leading · loved by parents · hand-picked · award-winning · transformational.* Each one is dead on the page.
- **No comparison ads naming competitors.** Kumon, Mathnasium, Khan, AI tutors, Wyzant — never named. We don't punch at strawmen. ~16% of trial parents have a tutor; we're not in their fight.
- **No "fun," "gamified," "engaging" as the lead claim.** The aspirational mother is buying rigor, not novelty. Gamification frames hurt CPTD in US data.
- **No offers, discounts, or limited-time framing in the close.** Free 1:1 class is the close. Always.
- **No multi-USP cards.** One USP per asset. If two USPs both seem essential, you have two assets, not one.
- **Never say "MathFit" without showing what it looks like.** Repetition without definition fails. Show the parent a child being clear, applying, or staying confident — then name it.
- **No India-specific cues in non-Indian markets.** ₹ pricing, Bangalore references, Hindi voice-over, Indian tutor visuals — these belong in NRI US and India creative only. Never in ANZ, UK, MEA non-Indian.

## 10 · The 100% accuracy checklist

Paste into every brief. Every asset must answer YES to all nine before it goes to design.

- [ ] **Goal** — which of the 5 parent goals (grades · acceleration · concept depth · competition · SAT)?
- [ ] **Dimension** — which ONE of Clarity / Application / Confidence?
- [ ] **Mechanism line** — the one sentence that ties the goal to the dimension, in the parent's language?
- [ ] **Coach is visible** — by name, by tenure, or by what she specifically remembers about the child?
- [ ] **Fletch length** — long / mid / short variant chosen?
- [ ] **CTA** — "Book a free 1:1 class" (default) or "Free SAT/strategy call" (HS only)?
- [ ] **Bleached-language scrub** — zero instances of *personalized · trusted · world-class · best · proven · loved · #1 · top-rated · industry-leading*?
- [ ] **Comparison scrub** — zero references to Kumon, Mathnasium, Khan, AI tutors, "tutoring centers"?
- [ ] **The sister test** — would the headline survive a mother texting her sister tomorrow?

If any box is unchecked, the asset doesn't ship. No exceptions for deadlines, channel briefs, or A/B variants.

## 11 · The closing test

Every claim, every ad, every product update, every campaign passes through one question:

> **Does this build the MathFit Score, or does it confuse it?**

If it builds — ship. If it confuses — kill. That single test, applied consistently across the next eighteen months, is the difference between a positioning doc and a brand worth a billion.
