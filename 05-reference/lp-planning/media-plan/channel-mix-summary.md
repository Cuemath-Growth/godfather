## What this means for the LP writer

| Channel type | LP voice cue |
|---|---|
| **Meta BAU + Google performance** | Sharp hook, message-match the ad, hard CTA, social proof, FAQ |
| **Brand Marketing (Meta/YouTube reach)** | Story-led, no hard CTA, awareness over conversion, broader voice. *Different LP, often a brand page not a trial booking page.* |
| **LinkedIn (India HS/IB)** | Formal, parent-as-professional, achievement-anchored. Reference IB/IGCSE explicitly. Higher trust signals (logos, certifications). |
| **TikTok (UAE, India, US NRI)** | Vertical-native. The LP must feel like a continuation of the TikTok hook, not a desktop site. Heavy visuals, short copy blocks. |
| **Taboola (US NRI, India)** | Native discovery → curiosity opens, then sharp anchor. The LP must justify the click immediately (the Taboola headline was vague by design). |
| **WhatsApp (CTWA India)** | LP should handoff to WhatsApp seamlessly — primary CTA = "Talk to us on WhatsApp," not "Book a class." |
| **Instant Forms (Meta IF)** | Often skips the LP. When LP is the destination, the form is the hero — minimal scroll, form above the fold. |

---

## Channel-experiment voice rule

When the Context Card destination is an **experimental channel** (LinkedIn, TikTok, Taboola), the writer should bias toward the **Wild variant** as the primary, because experimental channels are *for learning,* not optimisation. Safe variant is for BAU performance channels.
