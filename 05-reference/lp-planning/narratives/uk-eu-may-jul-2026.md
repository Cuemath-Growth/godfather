## Key context

- **KS2 SATs (Year 6)** — May (already concluded by May 10 in most years). Use as a *just-finished-exam* trigger early May.
- **GCSE / A-Level exams** — May–Jun. Year 11 / Year 13 high-anxiety windows.
- **11+ exam** — September. Summer is the prep window. Two-month framing in June lines up with families starting prep.
- **Summer break** — late Jul to early Sep — longest break, peak summer-slump moment.
