# Trial Mastery — AU + UK Parent Archetypes & Objections

**Source:** https://cuemath-row.github.io/trial-mastery/ — internal tutor refresher module. Built from analysing hundreds of real Australian and British trials.

**Use this when writing LPs for AU or UK.** Same way the ME brief is used for ME LPs.

---

## Australia — 5 Parent Archetypes

| Archetype | Trigger | Conversion rate | LP voice cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NAPLAN Mum** | NAPLAN approaching or recent results | — | Specific test prep, score-band language |
| **Switcher** | Previous tutor/centre didn't work | 24% | "What didn't work last time" framing |
| **Panic Parent** | Sudden grade drop, report card shock | — | Urgency + diagnosis-first language |
| **OC / Selective Chaser** | Year 5–7 entry tests | — | Scholarship/selective entry hooks |
| **NRI Returner** | Indian-Australian families (900K+ community) | — | Indian rigour + Australian-curriculum-aware |

**Curricula to acknowledge:** Australian, NSW, Victorian, WA.
**Tests:** NAPLAN (May, Years 3/5/7/9), OC, Selective Schools, private scholarships.

## UK — 5 Parent Archetypes

| Archetype | Trigger | LP voice cue |
|---|---|---|
| **11+ Mum** | Selective grammar school entry | Selective-entry, prep-window urgency |
| **KS2 SATs Mum** | Year 6 SATs in May | Test-prep, results day framing |
| **GCSE Crisis Parent** | Year 10–11 panic, mocks gone wrong | Diagnostic + recovery + exam-window urgency |
| **Indie Scholarship Chaser** | 7+/8+/13+ for independent schools | Scholarship language, prestige hooks |
| **NRI Returner** | Indian-British families | Indian rigour + British-curriculum-aware |

**Home nations:** England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland — voice differs.
**Tests:** KS2 SATs (May), 11+ (Sep), 7+/8+/13+, GCSE (May–Jun), A-Level (May–Jun).
**Competitors named (never mention by name in copy):** Atom, MyTutor, Third Space Learning, Kumon, Explore Learning.

**Copy rule:** UK uses "maths" not "math." Always.

## Common Objections Across AU + UK (7 patterns)

The Trial Mastery module trains tutors on 7 predictable objections. The same objections show up in LP intent — pre-empt them in FAQ and proof blocks.

1. Price — "How much per month?" → fee language, never "price"; tenure-based discount
2. Format — "Is online really as good as in-person?"
3. Teacher quality — "Will my child get a senior tutor?"
4. Curriculum match — "Do you know NAPLAN / KS2 SATs / GCSE?"
5. Time commitment — "Can my child handle another commitment?"
6. Long-term lock-in — "Try one month first" — start with 3-month base, longer = bigger discount
7. Spouse / family decision — "I'll discuss with my husband" — needs a WhatsApp summary equivalent on LP (downloadable parent doc)

## Year-Group Adaptations

**Australia:** K–2, 3–5, 6–8 (AU LPs **stop at Year 8** — no Year 11–12 unless explicitly briefed for HS).
**UK:** KS1, KS2, KS3 — and GCSE/A-Level when HS brief applies.

---

## How the LP writer uses this

When the Context Card market is **AUS** or **UK**:
1. Match audience field to one of the 5 archetypes above
2. Pull the LP voice cue for that archetype
3. Build the FAQ around the 7 objections — at minimum, address: price, format, teacher quality, curriculum match
4. Use **fee** not **price**. Use **maths** not **math** for UK.
5. For NRI Returner archetype in either market, the LP can reference Indian rigour + local curriculum awareness
