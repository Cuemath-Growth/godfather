## Step 2 — Sales Call Analysis: Goals + Objections

### On the Call Type Question

The 621 recordings span all call types. For ad creative purposes, the pre-trial inquiry calls (parent calling in cold before the trial is booked) are the highest signal — that is unfiltered parent motivation before any engagement with the product.

Breakdown of what was analyzed (Mar–Apr 2026):

| Call type | What it captures | Relevance for ad copy |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Pre-trial inquiry / slot booking | Parent calling in cold. Unfiltered goal. | **Primary source** |
| Trial reminder | Logistics — confirming attendance | Not relevant |
| Post-trial follow-up | Friction before paying | Useful for objection pre-emption in copy |
| Renewals | Existing students extending | Not relevant for cold ads |

## Step 3 — Creative Brief: 4 New Meta Statics

*Ankit's 4 best-performing creatives are the baseline. Each brief shows: current hook → new hook → why → parent language that drives it.*

**Rules that apply to all 4:**
- Pricing: **Starting from 60 AED/class** — not "25% off," not "12+3"
- Always **1:1**
- CTA: **Book a FREE class**
- Child photos: engaged and happy — never stressed
- **Trustpilot 4.9+** on all 4
- IB / Cambridge curriculum callout on Creatives 3 and 4

---

### Creative 1 — Generational Fear
**Target:** Indian / CBSE / British mothers | **Format:** Yellow bg, family photo

**Ankit's current hook:**
> *"Parents believed offline was better — until they saw Cuemath's 1:1 online math tutoring."*

**Why change it:** This overcomes an objection (online vs offline). The data shows a deeper, higher-intent trigger: parents who carry their own math anxiety and don't want to pass it on. That parent has already self-sold — the ad just needs to name the feeling.

**New headline:**
> *"I was scared of math. My child doesn't have to be."*

**Sub-copy:**
> 1:1 Online Math Tutoring — builds real confidence, not just exam scores.
> Starting from 60 AED/class.

**CTA:** Book a FREE class today

---

### Creative 2 — The Confidence Gap
**Target:** IB + CBSE parents, grade 4–7 | **Format:** Orange bg, child with headphones

**Ankit's current hook:**
> *"1:1 Personalized Online Math Tutoring, Built Around Your Child"*

**Why change it:** This is a benefit statement. The data shows parents scroll past benefits — they stop at problems they recognize. "Good at school, freezes on unfamiliar questions" is the most precisely stated fear in the paid parent data. It names the symptom before offering the solution.

**New headline:**
> *"Good at school. Freezes when the question is different."*

**Sub-copy:**
> Cuemath's 1:1 tutors don't just teach math — they rebuild how kids think about it.
> Starting from 60 AED/class.

**CTA:** Book a FREE 1:1 class

---

### Creative 3 — IB/Cambridge Foundation *(Highest priority)*
**Target:** IB + Cambridge parents | **Format:** Warm gradient bg, child at desk

**Ankit's current hook:**
> *"1:1 Live Math Classes for Strong Foundations"*

**Why change it:** Ankit's hook is a claim. IB parents in the data already speak this language — the shift is to reflect it back as a question. IB parents respond to intellectual challenge, not reassurance. 36% conversion rate vs 14% for other boards. This creative will drive disproportionate results.

**New headline:**
> *"Math builds on math. Is your child's foundation strong enough?"*

**Sub-copy:**
> 1:1 tutoring aligned to IB and Cambridge — identifies gaps before they compound.
> Starting from 60 AED/class.

**Curriculum badges:** [ IB ]  [ Cambridge ]  [ British ]

**CTA:** Book a FREE 1:1 class

---

### Creative 4 — Social Proof / Retargeting
**Target:** Warm retargeting — anyone who saw Creatives 1/2/3 | **Format:** Yellow/cream bg, quote card

**Ankit's current hook:**
> *"1:1 Online Math Tutoring for Strong Foundations"*

**Why change it:** Creative 4 is a retargeting creative — the audience has already seen the brand. A third product claim doesn't move them. A real parent quote does. "You gave her confidence in herself" is 6 words that do what three benefit bullets cannot.

**New headline:**
> *"You gave her confidence in herself."*
