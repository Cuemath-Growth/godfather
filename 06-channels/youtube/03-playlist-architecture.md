# Playlist Architecture & Channel Copy

## SCOPE FENCE

Organic channel copy. No RSA character limits, no Meta limits, no CPTD gates. [[forbidden-patterns]] and the [brand bible](https://cuemath-brand-book.netlify.app/) apply in full. Any number used here is gated on decision D5 in [[00-channel-revamp-brief]] §7 — both variants drafted below.

---

## The principle

**A playlist is a route, not a folder.**

Playlists autoplay. That makes them the only place on YouTube where you control *sequence* — which means a playlist is the closest thing the channel has to a funnel. Position 1 recruits. Positions 3–4 are where a CTA lands hardest, because by then the parent has invested twenty minutes and is warm. Position 8 is for people who were already sold at position 2.

Three more things playlists do that folders don't:

- **They rank in search** — YouTube's and Google's. A playlist title is keyword real estate.
- **They are the channel's shelf.** Section order on the channel page is a merchandising decision, and it is the first thing a new parent sees under the trailer.
- **They keep a viewer in the room.** A parent who lands on one explainer and leaves is worth a fraction of one who walks a route.

---

## Naming rules

1. Parent search language. Never internal taxonomy.
2. Front-load the keyword — playlist titles get truncated and they get indexed.
3. **US grade vocabulary.** `6th Grade`, never `Class 6` or `Year 6`. This is a geography signal.
4. ~55 characters visible on mobile. Write for that.
5. Never a framework name. No FUAR, no MathFit dimensions, no "productive struggle."
6. `Coach` in playlist titles — brand surface. `Tutor` permitted where a parent would search it (see *How to Choose a Math Tutor* below — that is the lexical-match exception, pending D6).

## The playlist set

Twelve playlists across four funnel tiers, plus one fenced.

### Tier 1 — Recruit (reach, top of funnel)

**`Math at Home: What Actually Helps`**
> What to do at the kitchen table when the homework stalls — and what to stop doing. Practical, grade by grade, from coaches who do this every day.
> Book a free 1-on-1 class → [LP]

**`Word Problems, Fractions & the Hard Parts`**
> The four or five topics that stall most kids, explained the way a coach would explain them 1:1 — slowly, and with the reasoning left in.

**`Math by Grade: What Kids Master When`**
> What your child is expected to master in each grade, aligned with US Common Core — and how to tell where they actually are.

### Tier 2 — Trust (consideration)

**`Inside a Cuemath Class`**
> Real 1-on-1 sessions, lightly edited. Watch a coach ask the next question instead of giving the answer.
> Book a free 1-on-1 class → [LP]

**`Meet the Coaches`**
> The people who stay. Coaches talking about the kids they've taught for three years and what changed.

**`How to Choose a Math Tutor`**
> What to ask, what to watch for in a trial class, and how to tell the difference between a tutor who explains and a coach who builds a thinker.

### Tier 3 — Convert (bottom of funnel)

**`Parents, One Year In`**
> Parents on what actually changed — a year, two years, three years with the same coach. Their words, not ours.
> Book a free 1-on-1 class → [LP]

**`AMC 8, MATHCOUNTS & Competition Prep`**
> How kids prepare for AMC 8, MATHCOUNTS and Olympiad — inside the same program as their school math, with the same coach.

**`SAT & AP Math: The Long Game`**
> Grades 9–12. Building the reasoning that carries through Pre-Calc, AP Calculus and the SAT — not test tricks.

### Tier 4 — Brand (subscribers, halo)

**`Hard Problems`**
> Students, real places, real problems. Math is the tool, never the subject. *(Hero documentary series — see the YouTube format manual.)*

**`Thinking in an AI World`**
> AI calculates. Humans have to think. What that changes about how kids should learn math.

### Fenced — off the funnel path

**`Math Curiosities`**
> Unfeatured. Not in any channel section. Holds the ~10 strongest legacy enthusiast videos so existing inbound links don't break. Everything else in the legacy library gets unlisted. Per decision D2.

## Channel shelf — section order

For a **non-subscriber** (the order that matters):

1. **Channel trailer** — 60–90s, parent-facing
2. `Inside a Cuemath Class` — highest-intent format, highest on the shelf
3. `Math at Home: What Actually Helps` — broadest recruit
4. `Math by Grade: What Kids Master When`
5. `Parents, One Year In`
6. `How to Choose a Math Tutor`
7. `AMC 8, MATHCOUNTS & Competition Prep`
8. `SAT & AP Math: The Long Game`
9. `Meet the Coaches`
10. `Hard Problems`
11. `Thinking in an AI World`
12. Shorts shelf
13. `Word Problems, Fractions & the Hard Parts`

`Math Curiosities` appears in **no** section.

For a **returning subscriber:** spotlight the most recent long-form, then `Inside a Cuemath Class`.

**Why intent sits above reach:** the shelf is not a reach surface — anyone reading it has already clicked the channel. Front-load what converts and let search and suggested carry the recruit content.

> Cuemath is a 1-on-1 math program for kids in grades K–12. One coach, who stays with your child for years.
>
> On this channel: what changes when a kid stops memorising and starts reasoning. Grade-by-grade explainers, real class sessions, and parents telling you what actually happened.

>
> Book a free 1-on-1 class → [LP]

### Variant B — with brand-bible numbers *(needs D5)*

> Cuemath is a 1-on-1 math program for kids in grades K–12. One coach, who stays with your child for years.
>
> 97.2% of parents say their child improved. Years with the same coach is how.
>
> On this channel: grade-by-grade explainers, real class sessions, and parents telling you what actually changed.
>
> Aligned with US Common Core. Making Kids MathFit™.
>
> Book a free 1-on-1 class → [LP]

Both drop the current positioning line — *"Math is simple, fun, beautiful and easy to master"* — which breaks three brand-bible rules at once (`easy`, `fun` outside K–2, and `simple` as a claim about the subject).

### Banner line

`One coach. Every class. Making Kids MathFit™.`

Close to the locked brand-film close card but not the card itself — the banner is a persistent brand surface, not an end frame, so it takes the shortened form.

## Channel trailer — Video Card

Marquee briefs; Forge writes. Per [[02-skills/format-manuals/youtube-video|youtube-video.md]] and the brand bible's 3-second rule.

```
VIDEO:        Channel trailer — parent-facing
FORMAT:       Hub-adjacent, 60-90s
PRIMARY KPI:  Subscribe rate from channel page · trailer completion
NOT JUDGED ON:Views, conversion
AUDIENCE:     US parents, grades 3-10, NRI-leaning
RECRUITS:     A parent deciding whether this channel is for them.
              Repels enthusiasts within the first 5 seconds — deliberately.
BRAND BEATS:  B2 Mechanism leads (coach who stays) + B3 MathFit. No B1 — this is brand
REGISTER:     Mixed; lean K-5 Warmth in the open, 6-12 Expertise in the close
SPINE:        The same coach, long enough to change how a kid thinks.
ROUTE:        Channel page · end screen → Inside a Cuemath Class · LP with utm yt_organic_trailer
FORGE NEEDS:  VO + on-screen text. First 3 seconds: no logo, no title, no "this is an ad."
              Open on a human moment — a kid pausing over a problem, a coach waiting.
              Cuemath is not named until after the parent feels something.
              No equations on screen in the first 10 seconds.
```

## Templates

### Video description

```
[Line 1 — the parent benefit, in the parent's words. This is visible above "…more".]
[Line 2 — Book a free 1-on-1 class → LP link with UTM]

—
[2-3 sentences: what this video covers, in search language. Grade level named.]

Chapters:
00:00 …

Cuemath is a 1-on-1 math program for grades K-12 — one coach, who stays with your
child for years. Aligned with US Common Core. Making Kids MathFit™.

Playlists:
Inside a Cuemath Class → [link]
Math by Grade → [link]
```

Only the first two lines are visible before "…more." They carry the benefit and the link. Everything after is for search.

### End screen (max 4 elements, last 20s)

1. Next video — the next position in the same playlist
2. Playlist — the route this video belongs to
3. Subscribe
4. Link — LP with UTM *(conversion-format videos only)*

### Pinned comment

One line + the LP link. On conversion-format videos, phrase it as the next step a parent would actually take, never as a sales line.
