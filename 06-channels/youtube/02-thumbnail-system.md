# YouTube Thumbnail System

## SCOPE FENCE

Organic YouTube thumbnails. **Meta's <20% text-on-image rule does not apply** — wrong platform. Neither do RSA character limits. What applies in full: [[forbidden-patterns]] and the brand bible (colour, photography, casting, attire) and [[forbidden-patterns]] §1.5.

---

## The law

**The thumbnail is not decoration. It is the only targeting instrument organic YouTube gives you.**

It does not attract viewers — it *selects* them. An equation selects someone who enjoys equations. A parent at a kitchen table selects a parent. The old thumbnails were not bad; they were aimed at the wrong person and they hit.

Two consequences that govern everything below:

1. **Every thumbnail must be able to name who it repels.** If it can't, it isn't targeting.
2. **Title and thumbnail are one two-part sentence.** Title carries the specific — grade, topic, situation. Thumbnail carries the stake or the feeling. Repeating the title on the thumbnail throws away half the instrument.

## Technical spec

| | |
|---|---|
| Canvas | 1280 × 720 (16:9), <2MB, PNG or JPG |
| **Design and approve at 210 × 118px** | Mobile suggested-feed size. If the words are unreadable there, they do not exist |
| Safe zones | Keep bottom-right 20% clear (duration stamp) · bottom 15% clear on Shorts covers (title overlay) · avoid the centre 10% on anything that may autoplay (play button) |
| Focal points | **One.** Two only for the split-screen coach/child composition |
| Face size | 25–40% of frame, on the third opposite the text block |
| Text | Max **4 words.** 2–3 is better. Never a sentence, never two competing text blocks |

**Consistency is a compounding asset.** Same accent treatment, same type, same crop logic across every upload means the channel reads as one shelf and a returning parent recognises us in a crowded feed before reading a word. This is the brand bible's *Hidden Logic* applied to a thumbnail grid.

## Composition matrix — who is in frame

Per the brand bible. Note the hard constraint: **coach and child are never in the same physical room.** The split-screen is the brand's online signature, and it rules out the obvious "coach sitting beside child" shot.

| Video format | In frame | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| Parent-decision long-form | **Parent alone** (late 30s–40s) | At a kitchen table or laptop, mid-thought. Looking slightly off-camera. Not smiling |
| Grade / topic explainer | **Coach alone** | Home office, mid-writing with a real pen, looking at the screen — not the camera |
| Session glimpse | **Coach + child, split-screen** | Kid one side, coach the other, shared whiteboard between. Never one room |
| Kid outcome | **Child alone** | Mid-work at a laptop. *The moment before understanding* — not the moment of triumph |
| Parent testimonial | **Parent + child, same room** — permitted | Home, side by side, child working, parent watching. The ban is coach + child, not parent + child |
| Hub film (3–6 min) | One human, no brand | A single human moment. ≤2 words of text, or none |
| Shorts cover | Coach or child | Vertical. Face in the upper third, text in the lower third, bottom 15% clear |

**Light signature, non-negotiable on every frame:** warm, soft, directional morning window light · 4800–5200K · gentle 2:1 contrast · 45° from upper-left.

**Environment as character:** wood, paper, books, a plant, a lived-in room. Never an institutional classroom, never a clinical desk.

## Casting

Straight from the brand bible — reproduced because thumbnails are where casting is most visible:

- **Mix:** 40% South Asian / NRI · 40% other Asian · 20% non-Asian. Lead face matches the targeted cell; supporting cast always includes one non-Asian face.
- **K–5 content:** women coaches only. **Middle + HS:** 50/50. **HS:** slight male lean.
- **Coach age:** late 30s default.
- **Children:** age-appropriate to grade tier. Faces may be shown — real, released talent only, never AI-generated.
- **Attire:** smart casuals, global professional. Crisp shirt or blouse, solid colours, warm neutrals or a gold accent. **Never saris, suits, kurtas, or Indian-wear.**

That last rule is the same brand logic as the `Indian Tutors` hard ban, extended to wardrobe. Wardrobe is a positioning signal, and on a thumbnail it is read before any word.

## Colour and type

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Accent | Sunlit Gold `#F4AB52` — **one element only.** An underline bar, a single ring, or one highlighted word. Never a flood, never an arrow |
| Type | Warm Charcoal `#292827` on a Warm Off-White `#FAF8F5` panel, **or** off-white type on a darkened area of the photo |
| Ground | 70%+ neutral. Gold is punctuation |
| Banned | Pure black · pure white · cool greys · red accents of any kind (fear signal) · gradients across the whole frame · drop shadows heavy enough to read as a sticker |
| Typeface | Athletics for the thumbnail word — it is a heading, not UI |

## Copy rules

1. **Max 4 words. Aim for 2–3.**
2. **Never repeat the title.** Title = the specific. Thumbnail = the stake.
3. **Parent's language, not ours.** Would a parent say this at school pickup? If not, cut it.
4. **US grade vocabulary.** `6th Grade`, never `Class 6` or `Year 6`. Grade words are a geography signal the algorithm reads.
5. **No framework language.** No FUAR, no MathFit dimensions, no "productive struggle." ([[forbidden-patterns]] §3.3 — internal-only.)
6. **No close-card lines in the thumbnail slot.** *"Same Cuemath Coach. Every Single Class."* is an end-frame line. Hoisting it to a thumbnail is the slot mismatch in [[forbidden-patterns]] §3.2.
7. **No numbers as hooks.** No stats, no percentages, no "99%."
8. **No `X — not Y` constructions.** [[forbidden-patterns]] §6.1.
9. **`Coach` on the thumbnail, always.** Thumbnail copy is a brand surface. `Tutor` is permitted only in the title, for search match (pending D6 sign-off).
10. **Lowercase is allowed and often better.** Sentence case reads calmer than title case, and calm is the brand.

| Thumbnail copy | Words | Recruits / repels |
|---|---|---|
| `before you sign up` | 4 | Parent actively evaluating tutoring · repels casual browsers |
| `what actually changed` | 3 | Outcome-skeptical parent · repels enthusiasts |
| `one year in` | 3 | Long-horizon parent, B2 mechanism · repels quick-fix seekers |
| `three years, one coach` | 4 | The retention story, B2 · repels shoppers |
| `ask this one question` | 4 | Parent looking for agency · repels students |
| `6th grade changes` | 3 | Middle-school-cliff parent, highest-intent cell |
| `what good looks like` | 4 | Parent who can't evaluate a tutor · repels price shoppers |
| `he explained it back` | 4 | Observed-behaviour proof, brand-book preferred form |
| `she showed her work` | 4 | Same, K–5 register |
| `the pause before` | 3 | *Moment before understanding.* Hub film / brand slot |

### Session glimpse

| Thumbnail copy | Words | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `inside a real class` | 4 | Highest-intent format. Says exactly what it is |
| `45 minutes, unedited` | 3 | Transparency signal. Only if it is actually unedited |
| `the coach waits` | 3 | Brand-book direction: *show the coach listening more than speaking* |
| `watch her think` | 3 | Kid-outcome framing, no claim |

### Grade / topic explainer

| Thumbnail copy | Words | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `fractions, actually explained` | 3 | Search-adjacent, parent language |
| `word problems, step one` | 4 | Names the #1 parent-reported stall point without remediation framing |
| `8th grade algebra` | 3 | Pure search signal + geography signal |
| `why this trips kids up` | 4 | Curiosity without puzzle framing |

### Parent testimonial

| Thumbnail copy | Words | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `what her mom saw` | 4 | Parent-voice frame. Single-kid pronouns are correct here — [[forbidden-patterns]] §3.1 |
| `two kids, same coach` | 4 | Sibling story, brand-book proof format |
| `year two` | 2 | Restraint. Works because the photo carries it |

### Banned from the copy bank

`math is easy` · `easy math` · `crush math` · `math made simple` · `math is fun` (outside K–2) · `struggling?` · `behind in math?` · `from C to A+` · `top 1%` bare · `unlock potential` · `#1` · `99% can't solve this` · `limited seats` · `genius` · `prodigy` · anything with `AI-powered` as the claim.

## Banned thumbnail patterns

The first one is the whole reason this document exists.

1. **Equations, chalkboards, whiteboards full of symbols, geometry diagrams as the hero.** This is the mechanism that acquired the wrong audience. No exceptions — not even for explainer content, where the temptation is highest. If the video is about fractions, show the *child* looking at fractions, not the fractions.
2. **Puzzle or riddle framing.** "Can you solve this?" recruits solvers. We do not want solvers; we want their parents.
3. **Open-mouth shock faces, pointing, wide-eyed reaction.** Violates *Considered Calm*.
4. **Red arrows, red circles, red anything.** Brand book bans red-accent fear signals outright.
5. **Countdowns, timers, "only 3 left."** Manufactured urgency, banned in copy and in image.
6. **Numbers as clickbait.** `99% CAN'T SOLVE THIS`, `IQ 140+`.
7. **Cartoon characters, mascots, infantilising illustration.**
8. **Staged smiles, thumbs-up, stock-photo energy.** Brand book names this as a failure mode by name.
9. **AI-generated faces.** Brand book bans "the AI look" — uncanny lighting, over-smooth skin, plastic surfaces. Thumbnails are the most-scrutinised frame we publish; a synthetic face here costs more trust than it saves in production.
10. **Saris, kurtas, suits, Indian-wear on the coach.**
11. **Institutional classroom backdrops.** Wrong category — we are not a school or a tuition centre.
12. **More than 4 words, or two text blocks competing.**
13. **The Cuemath logo large on the thumbnail.** Wordmark small or absent. A logo-forward thumbnail reads as an ad and suppresses click-through; the brand lands in the video, not on the poster.

## QA checklist — before any thumbnail ships

- [ ] Readable at 210 × 118px
- [ ] ≤4 words, does not repeat the title
- [ ] One focal point (or a deliberate split-screen)
- [ ] No equations, symbols, or chalkboard anywhere in frame
- [ ] Coach and child not in the same physical room
- [ ] Warm morning light, 45° upper-left, no cool grey, no red
- [ ] Gold on exactly one element
- [ ] Attire compliant — no Indian-wear
- [ ] Casting compliant for the grade tier
- [ ] Real photography, not AI-generated faces
- [ ] Bottom-right 20% clear
- [ ] **Named who this repels** — written down, not assumed
- [ ] Scanned against [[forbidden-patterns]] §5 banned-phrase list

## Re-packaging existing videos

Free inventory, and the fastest visible movement in the whole revamp: YouTube re-tests packaging on old uploads. Priority order —

1. Existing videos with genuine parent relevance → new thumbnail + new title on the new system. Keep the video, change the poster.
2. Existing videos that are enthusiast-only → fence (unlist or de-feature). Do not re-package what cannot be re-aimed.
3. Anything with a symbol-wall thumbnail that is staying public → re-thumbnail regardless of its topic. Old thumbnails keep recruiting from the sidebar for as long as they are visible.
