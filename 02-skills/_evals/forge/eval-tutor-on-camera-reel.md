## Brief / Input

> `/write video`
>
> Format: in-house tutor-on-camera Meta Reel (Q&A format)
> Topic: parent question — "my Year 5 still uses fingers to add 9 + 6, how do you handle that?"
> Surface: Meta Reels (brand)
> Tutor: Cuemath coach speaking on camera, recounting a real parent quote
> Length: ~30-45 seconds VO
> Audience: AU / NRI / India parents of grades 3-7

## Expected output — checkable criteria

The script must hit ALL seven beats in the May 13 calibration:

1. **Topic-led opener IN A SENTENCE** — not a chyron, not a setup line. The first VO line names the parent question conversationally.
2. **HOOK = parent quote with 2 concrete examples** — the tutor recounts the parent's actual quote, and the parent gives TWO specific examples (not one, not zero). Format: "A mom told me last week, 'my kid still does X. And Y.'"
3. **MECHANISM = live dialogue** — the script shows a beat of the tutor + kid talking, not the tutor describing the method abstractly.
4. **MathCanvas AND MathGym named** — both product surfaces appear by name (not "our digital whiteboard" / "our practice tool" — the verbatim product names).
5. **Cue-don't-tell explicit** — the script demonstrates the cue-don't-tell technique in the live dialogue beat (tutor asks instead of tells), not just mentions it.
6. **OUTCOME = specific fear-resolution verb** — one of: *freeze, stare, pause, panic, blank, blank-out*. The outcome line names the fear behaviour the kid no longer does. Generic outcomes ("confident", "happy", "loves math") fail.
7. **Close card = May 10 lock VERBATIM** — *"Same Cuemath Coach. Every Single Class. Making Your Child MathFit™."* End-frame placement. NO paraphrase. This IS a brand film context per forbidden-patterns §6.4 (brand reels = close card slot).

## Voice-frame check

- Tutor is speaking ON CAMERA — single-kid pronouns ("she", "her Year 5") are OK in the parent-quote recounting (parent voice).
- Tutor's own narration should stay in tutor-mentor voice (categorical), not Cuemath-marketing voice.
- FUAR pillars and MathFit dimensions are BANNED in VO. MathFit™ as a brand term is OK (in the close card). Dimensions go on-screen only.

## What this catches

- Topic-led opener missing → script reads as method-essay instead of conversation
- Single-example parent quote ("a mom told me her kid struggles") → not concrete enough
- Mechanism described instead of shown → loses the "live dialogue" texture that makes the reel work
- Product surfaces unnamed ("our app") → kills the specificity moat
- Generic outcomes ("more confident") → fails the fear-resolution-verb test
- Close card missing or paraphrased → brand-rule violation that needs ongoing audit
