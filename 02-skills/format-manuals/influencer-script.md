# Influencer / UGC script — format manual

The format manual for sponsored creator scripts — Asian-American Mom Creators, NRI lifestyle creators, mum-influencer accounts, and any UGC where the speaker is NOT Cuemath.

KPI is CPTD via creator audience trust × authentic story landing × clear next step. Influencer is the ONLY paid surface where the speaker isn't Cuemath — that changes everything about the discipline. **Brand discipline lives inside creator authority, not on top of it.**

---

## When to invoke

You are writing a creator script when ALL of these are true:
- Speaker = the creator, in her own life, in her own voice, to her own audience
- Surface = creator's own feed (Instagram Reel, YouTube Short / video, TikTok, etc.)
- Format = ~60-second vertical video script (sometimes longer for YouTube)
- Audience = creator's followers, who trust HER, not Cuemath

If the speaker is Cuemath (brand-voice video), use [[meta-reel.md]] or [[youtube-video.md]] — different physics.

---

## Physics of this surface

| Property | Reality |
|---|---|
| Speaker | The creator. She has authority because her audience trusts her, not us. |
| Trust contract | Her followers trust her recommendations because she sounds like herself. The moment she sounds scripted, the contract breaks. |
| Time budget | ~60 seconds typical. School-pickup pacing, not deck pacing. |
| Voice register | Creator-natural. NOT Cuemath brand voice. NOT advertising rhythm. |
| Authority anchor | Her real life — her actual kids, her actual school district, her actual story of finding the product. Authority breaks the moment we invent details. |
| Brand surface visibility | Cuemath is mentioned 1-2 times, named at proof + CTA. Body is HER, not us. |
| Editing latitude | Creators will reshape any script. Hand them a chassis with their voice intact, not a fully-written ad. |

## What's locked / what's free — fundamentally different from in-house

The mistake I keep making: applying the in-house Lock list to influencer scripts. **Don't.** Influencer locks are smaller and different.

### Locked (truthful brand discipline only)

- **Truthful CTA: "Book a free 1:1 class"** at the end. Phrasing can be creator-natural ("Book a free trial" / "Sign up for a free 1:1") — content is locked, exact phrasing has room.
- **No invented product terms.** Don't put words in the creator's mouth that aren't Cuemath features.
- **No invented life facts.** If the creator didn't say her kid was the 7th — don't write "I didn't want her to be the seventh." Verify every life-fact against the source brief.
- **MathFit™ trademark** on closing card when used (rare in influencer — only when creator's voice earns it).
- **Top 1% selectivity stat** as 1-in-100 (not 1-in-200 — outdated).
- **Tutor (not teacher) in body** when the role word is needed. "Coach" in closing card only if creator-voice naturally lands it.
- **No competitor names.** Creator can describe what didn't work ("the worksheets we tried") without naming the brand.
- **No fabricated offers / urgency / countdowns.** Truthful only.

### Free (creator authority owns everything else)

- Sentence rhythm, cadence, sentence length
- Opening hook (her real moment of noticing the kid's maths issue)
- Scene count, scene structure, transitions
- Vocabulary — including slang, fillers, em-dashes, vernacular phrases per market voice canon
- The exact phrasing of the brand mention (so long as it carries the moat)
- The closing line in HER voice (not the locked in-house close card)
- Whether she names her tutor, by tenure or memory
- Energy level — calm-observational vs animated-conversational, per creator's natural register
- Emojis, on-screen text overlay style, music choice

**No locked close card on influencer scripts.** That close lives in in-house content. A creator ending with *"Same Cuemath Coach. Every Single Class. Making Your Child MathFit™"* breaks her voice the moment it lands.

## The 5-step process (locked May 11, calibrated May 12)

Every creator script goes through these 5 steps, in order:

### Step 1 — Register sort (BEFORE writing)

What audience does this creator reach? Sort first.

| Register | Audience | Cuemath voice canon |
|---|---|---|
| **Asian-American Mom (non-NRI East Asian)** | Chinese / Korean / Filipino / Vietnamese American mothers, school-pickup peer audience | [[voice-asian-mom-creator]] — conversational, em-dashes, "actually," "anymore" |
| **NRI lifestyle creator** | Indian-American mothers, NRI community-trust audience | [[voice-us-nri-firstgen]] or [[voice-us-nri-secondgen]] depending on creator's gen |
| **Indian-Australian mum creator** | Indian-Australian audience | [[voice-au-indian]] |
| **UK Asian-origin mum creator** | UK British-Indian audience | [[voice-uk-british-indian]] |
| **India-resident creator** | India-domestic audience | [[voice-india-parent]] |
| **International expat / MEA** | UAE expat audience | [[voice-mea-mixed]] |

If you can't sort the register, stop and ask. Wrong-register scripts ship and fail.

### Step 2 — Pull the source brief

For first-time creators: the agency/agent brief. Verify creator's:
- Name, age, location, profession
- Kids' real names, ages, grades, schools
- Real anchor moment (when did she actually try Cuemath? What was the kid struggling with?)
- Her audience profile

**Never invent any of this.** If the brief doesn't have it, ask. If you can't get it, don't write.

### Step 3 — For repeat creators: pull the shipped script

Repeat creators REQUIRE the actual shipped script from `creative_assets.csv` per [[reference_influencer_script_process]]. Stop and ask if it's missing.

You're writing v2 of a v1 the creator has already shipped. v2 ≈ v1 + 5-10% targeted edits per [[feedback_minimum_edit_principle]] — NOT a rewrite. Diff before sending.

### Step 4 — Draft in her voice, not yours

Internalise the register from the creator's existing content + her ground-truth life facts. Write as if you ARE her, talking to her audience at school pickup.

Brand-discipline rules (CTA, MathFit trademark, no-invented-claims) are PRINCIPLES to express in her voice, not strings to paste. Per [[feedback_principles_not_templates]].

### Step 5 — Read-aloud test

Read the script aloud, as her, to a friend. If any sentence makes you sound like you're reading from a deck, that sentence breaks register. Rewrite.

Per [[reference_naina_tonal_canon_asian_mom_scripts]] — observations not rules. The canon is a way to NOTICE good register, not a checklist to satisfy.

## Variation axes — when generating multiple creator scripts in a campaign

If you're writing 4 creator scripts for the same campaign (e.g. 4 Asian-American Mom creators for the US DBS launch), differentiate at architecture level. Lock 4 brand-mandatory anchors:

1. CTA truthful
2. MathFit grounding (if used at all, only at close)
3. 1-in-100 selectivity (if mentioned)
4. No-invented-life-facts

Permute everything else across ≥ 4 dimensions per [[feedback_combinatorial_not_iterative]]:

| Axis | Variation examples |
|---|---|
| **Opening dynamic** | Forecast worry · celebration · trigger event · school-report moment · peer-comparison · kid quote |
| **Discovery story** | Heard from a friend · saw a post · Googled · came up in a group chat · tried multiple things first |
| **Scene structure** | 3-scene · 4-scene · 5-scene · single-frame monologue · split-screen comparison · before/after dyptich |
| **Evidence pillar** | Outcome (score/grade) · tenure (months/years) · memory (what the tutor remembers about the kid) · trial-class observation · the parent's own reversal |
| **Closing line shape** | "If your kid is X, try this" · "We're staying with it because Y" · "Honestly, this is what worked" · "Here's what surprised me" |

Surface-level phrasing changes ("she's quick" vs "she's fast") don't count. Different architecture or it's the same script.

## Per-market overrides

### India (India-resident creator)

| Field | India creator override |
|---|---|
| Voice canon | Vernacular-mixed (English-led with Hindi/regional phrases at emotional anchors) — like "samajh ke solve karti hai" |
| CTA | "Book a free 1:1 class" / "Free trial book karo" — phrasing creator-natural |
| Tutor / coach word | Tutor in body. Coach in close ONLY if creator-natural. "Cuemath teacher" sometimes used — flag to creator that "tutor" is preferred but accept her natural word. |
| Banned in body | "Indian-trained" framing · framework names · invented offers · negative bashing of local tutors |
| Allowed | Real Indian school context, Hindi/regional phrases, board exam references (if creator's kid is older), Olympiad / contest references |
| Audience trust anchor | School-mum-network · Indian education community · WhatsApp-shareable moment |

### US (3 sub-canons)

**First-gen NRI lifestyle creator (Texas-heavy, WhatsApp-led audience):**
- Community-trust register
- Vernacular Hindi/regional OK at emotional anchors
- NRI parent-vocabulary primary
- Temple/cultural-community references OK as life context (not brand framing)
- NO "Indian-trained" / "Indian rigour" framing
- CTA: "Book a free 1:1 class"

**Second-gen NRI lifestyle creator (Bay-heavy, peer-evidence audience):**
- Evidence-first register
- Less vernacular, more evidence-led (numbers, outcomes, comparisons)
- RSM-aware framing (parent has shopped around)
- Acceleration / advancement language
- NO "Indian-trained" framing
- CTA: "Book a free 1:1 class"

**Asian-American Mom Creator (Chinese/Korean/Filipino/Vietnamese, non-NRI):**
- Conversational school-pickup register per [[voice-asian-mom-creator]]
- Em-dashes, "actually," "anymore," conversational connectors
- No vernacular (her audience speaks English at home)
- Anchor in her real American suburban / cultural life
- CTA: "Book a free 1:1 class" — creator's natural phrasing OK

### UK (Settled UK Asian-origin mum creator)

| Field | UK creator override |
|---|---|
| Voice canon | UK-fluent register. British English (maths, Year 5/6, Mum, BrE spelling). Vernacular ONLY at emotional anchor moments. |
| CTA | "Book a free 1:1 class" |
| Tutor word | "tutor" in body. "Tuition" sometimes natural for UK mum creators. |
| Exam-prep references | 11+ · KS2 SATs · 7+/8+/13+ (K-8 cap — no GCSE/A-Level references) |
| Banned in body | "Indian-trained" anywhere · AmE spelling · "11+ specialist" framing |
| Audience trust anchor | UK mum-network, grammar-school discussion, school-WhatsApp groups |

### AU (Indian-Australian or East Asian Australian mum creator)

| Field | AU creator override |
|---|---|
| Voice canon | Per sub-segment: Indian-AU (vernacular OK at emotional moments) vs East Asian AU (pure AU-fluent, no vernacular) |
| CTA | "Book a free 1:1 class" |
| Exam-prep references | NAPLAN (in season) · OC · Selective · Scholarship (no NCEA, no PSLE) |
| Banned in body | "Indian-trained" · AmE spelling · NAPLAN out-of-season · "Selective specialist" framing |
| Audience trust anchor | AU diaspora community, school-pickup mums, state-specific (NSW/VIC) WhatsApp groups |

### MEA (Expat creator)

| Field | MEA creator override |
|---|---|
| Voice canon | International expat register — neutral English, no cultural markers |
| CTA | "Book a free 1:1 class" |
| Banned in body | South Asian cultural framing · "Indian-trained" · vernacular phrases · "Arab tutors" framing |

## CPTD gates — what to ship-test against

Before shipping a creator script:

1. **Creator's audience trust score** — historical engagement on past sponsored content vs organic. If trust is shaky, the script can't fix it.
2. **Read-aloud test passes** — most-leveraged gate. If you sound like you're reading from a deck, the script breaks the trust contract.
3. **Comparison against creator's last 5 organic posts** — does the script's voice match her natural register? Tone-mismatch = trust break.
4. **Has she been through the brief?** Creator approval before shoot = better-aligned shipped piece.
5. **Cell-weighted CPTD benchmark from Godfather** — what's the current best-performing influencer in this cell? If the new script doesn't credibly compete, don't ship a 5th similar variant.

## Failure modes — the recurring ones (catalogued across 14-draft sessions)

1. **Templating around a canonical example.** Naina rewrites Erden as a tone demo. You extract 12 observations. You apply them to 4 new scripts as a checklist. All 4 scripts sound identical. The canon is for developing ear, NOT a recipe.
2. **Brand-canon insertions into body.** Stamping *"Same Cuemath Coach. Every Single Class."* verbatim into creator body. That's an in-house close. Body uses creator's natural attribution.
3. **Framework names in parent VO.** "She has built fluency, understanding, reasoning..." — never spoken. Outcomes-as-observed-behaviour only.
4. **Inventing life facts.** Writing "I didn't want her to be the seventh" when the brief said 6 kids. Truth is always more specific than what you'll fabricate.
5. **Rewriting when minimum-edit applies.** Repeat creator's approved v1 gets a new lock; you rewrite the whole thing through that lock's lens. v(n+1) ≈ v(n) + 5-10% diff.
6. **Force-fitting tonal observations.** Naina demos em-dashes. You stuff em-dashes everywhere. That's not tone, that's parody.
7. **Applying NRI rigor register to non-NRI Asian-American audience.** "There's a layer of math most school curricula don't have time to reach..." sounds constructed to a Chinese-American school-pickup mum.
8. **Standalone explainer scenes.** "Whether your child is in K-12, Cuemath helps them..." — that's a broadcast intro, not a creator scene. Drop.
9. **Logical gaps preserved from original draft.** Original ends with "having something flexible like this has made a huge difference" — but nothing earlier established what flexible was. Either set it up OR replace the claim. Don't preserve gaps under "minimum edit."
10. **Performed emotion.** "Honestly that confidence? That's everything." If a real mum wouldn't say it to her friend at school pickup, cut it.
11. **CTA broken from hook.** Hook = "kids finish math homework in 15 minutes." CTA should mirror that question: "If your kid is fast at math, know for real where their understanding is at." Don't switch CTAs mid-script.
12. **MathFit in body.** Should live on closing card only when earned. Body uses outcome-as-observed-behaviour.
13. **Common endplate across 4 creator scripts.** Each creator has her own natural close. Don't stamp.
14. **Naming the coach as outcome source.** "Coach Aditi prepped 12 students" puts tutor as product. Outcomes attribute to Cuemath / the Cuemath Way. Coach visible by tenure or memory.

## Output checklist before ship

- [ ] Speaker = the creator, in her voice (read-aloud confirms)
- [ ] All life facts verified against source brief (no invention)
- [ ] Voice canon applied per audience cell
- [ ] CTA truthful: "Book a free 1:1 class" (or creator-natural phrasing of same)
- [ ] No invented product terms / no fabricated offers / no countdowns
- [ ] No competitor names
- [ ] No "Indian-trained" / "Indian rigour" framing
- [ ] No MathFit framework dimensions spoken in body
- [ ] No in-house locked close card stamped on the end
- [ ] Tutor (not teacher) in body; coach only if creator-natural at close
- [ ] No bare-name coach as outcome source — outcomes attribute to Cuemath
- [ ] If repeat creator: diff against v1 < 10%
- [ ] If first-time creator: source brief checked, no life-fact invention
- [ ] Read-aloud test passes (sounds like the creator, not a deck)
- [ ] Variants in same campaign architecturally distinct (≥ 4 axes differ)
- [ ] Per-market vocabulary, spelling, currency posture
- [ ] Coherence block filled — LP exists, vocabulary lock recorded
- [ ] Creator approval before shoot
