# YouTube Video — format manual

The format manual for YouTube video assets — TrueView pre-roll, in-feed video, Shorts, Hub films, Hero documentary series. One file covering all YouTube formats because they share the platform's core physics (long-form patience, sound-on by default, completion-rate-driven distribution).

KPI is CPTD via watch-time × completion × click-through. YouTube is the only paid surface where the parent is genuinely engaged with longer-form content; the format rewards depth that Meta + Google Search don't.

---

## When to invoke

You are writing YouTube video when ALL of these are true:
- Surface = YouTube (TrueView pre-roll, in-feed, Shorts, organic uploads)
- Speaker = Cuemath (in-house brand voice) — for influencer YouTube, use [[influencer-script.md]]
- Format = video, varying duration by sub-format

| Sub-format | Duration | Surface | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TrueView pre-roll (skippable)** | 15-30s (with 5s skip) | Before another video | Earn the 5-second-skip moment |
| **In-feed video ad** | 15-30s | YouTube feed | Earn the click |
| **Shorts** | up to 60s | Vertical Shorts feed | 3-sec hook physics, same as Reels |
| **Hub film (DBS)** | 3-6 min | Cuemath channel + organic + bi-weekly performance boost | Brand positioning, FUAR pillar anchor |
| **Hero documentary (DBS)** | 20-30 min/episode | Cuemath channel, 2 series/year × 5 episodes | Hard Problems series — flagship brand |

---

## Physics — YouTube vs other surfaces

| Property | Reality |
|---|---|
| Read context | Parent watching video, sound on default, longer attention span than Meta. Pre-roll = interrupted; in-feed = curiosity-led; Shorts = vertical-scroll passive. |
| Time budget | Varies wildly. Pre-roll: 5-sec-skip. Shorts: 3-sec hook. Hub film: 5-second hook earns 6-minute watch. Hero doc: title-thumbnail-first-30s earns the 20-minute commitment. |
| Sound | On by default (opposite of Meta). VO and dialogue carry weight that Reels can't. |
| Aspect | 16:9 horizontal (TrueView, in-feed, Hub, Hero) · 9:16 vertical (Shorts) |
| Algorithmic distribution | Watch-time × completion-rate × replay-rate × shares. Hub + Hero content benefits from algorithm; paid pre-roll buys the impression. |
| YouTube hook principle (DBS) | First 5 seconds must hook WITHOUT Cuemath, WITHOUT math, WITHOUT "this is an ad" — parent feels something first, earns the brand reveal |

## Sub-format mechanics

### TrueView pre-roll (paid skippable)

- 5-second skip moment is the gate
- Must communicate AT LEAST the hook before second 5
- Brand reveal can come after the skip moment (since skip-rate is heavily influenced by first 5s)
- 15-30s total length typical; 30s allows full 3-beat structure
- CTA appears in last 5s + companion banner
- Spec: 16:9 horizontal

### In-feed video ad (paid)

- Title + thumbnail earn the click
- 15-30s body, similar to TrueView but viewers self-selected
- 3-beat structure works full
- CTA card overlays at 70% watch-time mark

### Shorts (paid or organic)

- Same physics as [[meta-reel.md]] — 3-sec hook, vertical, sound-off default
- 60s max
- See meta-reel.md for full discipline

### Hub films (3-6 minutes — DBS bi-weekly)

DBS map: 13 hub films per year, one per FUAR pillar + positioning moment. Each film:

| Film # | Subject | Core idea | Opening frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Productive Struggle | The moment before giving up is the moment of growth | A child staring at a problem. Silence. No tutor intervention. |
| 2 | Fluency | Speed without understanding is fragile | A child who can explain why, not just how |
| 3 | Understanding | Real understanding outlasts the exam | Parent: "She explained it to me at the dinner table." |
| 4 | Application | Math is not a subject. It is a way of seeing. | A child noticing a pattern in something non-mathematical |
| 5 | Reasoning | The best students ask why. Always. | A child questioning a formula rather than accepting it |
| 6 | The Middle School Cliff | The moment parents miss | A mother watching her daughter struggle — for the first time |
| 7 | The Tutor Relationship | The coach doesn't give answers. They build thinkers. | A tutor asking a question. Waiting. The child working it out. |
| 8 | Earned Confidence | Confidence from capability, not praise | A child who doesn't check with the tutor before answering |
| 9 | Math Anxiety → Math Identity | The transformation is not about grades | A child who used to dread it, and now asks for the hard problem |
| 10 | The AI World | In an AI era, human judgment is non-negotiable | No math in the first minute. A story about a decision only a human can make. |
| 11 | The Parent's Role | The best thing you can do is ask "why" | A parent learning to ask differently |
| 12 | Long-Term vs. Short-Term | Tricks are fast. Thinking is permanent. | A child who failed a test but learned more from it than any A |
| 13 | What MathFit Looks Like at 16 | The payoff | A teenager navigating something hard — not math-related |

Each: 3-6 minutes. Live action. Verite documentary feel. No on-camera Cuemath until end-frame. Sample script structure in DBS p.11-13.

### Hero documentary (20-30 min — DBS, 2 series/year)

**Hard Problems** series. 10 episodes/year (5 per series, 2 series). Each episode: students (10-15 years old) travel to a real place, tackle a real problem with math as the tool. Brand never the subject. Math never the subject. The subject is how young people think about the world.

Episode list per DBS p.14-15:

| Series 1 | Location | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Ep 1 | Nevada Desert | How much water does a city actually need? |
| Ep 2 | Pacific Ocean | How much plastic is in the ocean? |
| Ep 3 | Old Delhi | Reduce pollution by 40% in one neighbourhood — how? |
| Ep 4 | Houston | The city floods every year. What would you do? |
| Ep 5 | Smithsonian | Build a museum that tells the story of the next 100 years. |

| Series 2 | Location | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Ep 6 | Amazon Rainforest | $1B and a year to stop deforestation. What's most efficient? |
| Ep 7 | Tokyo | City aging — 1 in 3 over 65 by 2050. Restructure how? |
| Ep 8 | Kibera, Nairobi | Education access at scale. What would it actually cost? |
| Ep 9 | CERN, Switzerland | What questions can humans not yet answer? |
| Ep 10 | No fixed location | Students design their own problem. Defend their math. |

End-of-episode tag (one screen): *"These students were made MathFit by people like [tutor name]. If your child has a hard problem waiting for them, Cuemath can help."*

## Lock / Free / Anchor — by sub-format

### TrueView pre-roll

**Locked:**
- First 5s without Cuemath / without math / without "this is an ad" (DBS YouTube hook principle)
- Locked close card at end (verbatim)
- CTA "Book a free 1:1 class" (HS US: diagnostic CTA)
- Single MathFit dimension (or none) — never two
- Coach visible (tenure or memory, not bare-name)

**Free:**
- Pre-skip hook structure
- Cut-down from hub film vs original short ad
- VO vs silent + caption
- Voice canon per market

### Hub films (3-6 min)

**Locked:**
- One named dynamic from DBS §8 (one of the 10)
- One FUAR pillar or positioning moment (one of the 13)
- First 5s without Cuemath / math / ad
- Locked end-frame: *Cuemath. Making kids MathFit.* + free-trial CTA card
- No Cuemath named until the end frame
- Documentary register (not advertising)

**Free:**
- Specific story details
- Cast
- Setting
- VO writer's voice
- Music / sound design (often silence works)
- Per-market voice canon (different hub films can be cut for different markets)

### Hero documentary (20-30 min)

**Locked:**
- Real students, real places, real problems
- Math as tool, not subject
- Brand invisible until end-tag
- End-tag verbatim format

**Free:**
- Episode-specific story
- Director's voice
- Editing rhythm
- Cast selection

### India
- TrueView active: limited (lower YouTube paid weight than Meta)
- Hub films / Hero films can be dubbed or subtitled in regional languages for organic distribution

- TrueView active: HIGH spend per DBS (Q3 + Q4 emphasis)

## CPTD gates

1. **5-second skip rate** (TrueView) — % who don't skip. Cell-weighted benchmark from Godfather.
2. **Watch-time** — average % completed
3. **Click-through to LP** — varies by sub-format
4. **Hub film completion rate** (organic) — 35%+ for 3-6 min is good
5. **Hero doc subscriber acquisition** — long-tail brand metric

---

## Failure modes — YouTube-specific

1. **Cuemath named in first 5 seconds of TrueView.** DBS rule violation. Skip rate spikes.
2. **Hub film with on-camera "Hi, this is Cuemath."** Breaking the fourth wall, kills the documentary register. DBS bans.
3. **Pizza-multiplication / childish visuals on middle-school+ audience.** DBS bans for the 6-12 audience.
4. **Talking-head testimonial without product shown.** DBS rule — show the session, the screen, the actual experience.
5. **Generic edtech language in VO.** "Unlock potential," "world-class," etc. Bleached.
6. **Framework names spoken in VO.** FUAR / MathFit dimensions banned in parent VO per [[feedback_frameworks_not_in_vo]].
7. **Hub film as a 6-minute ad.** Should feel like a short film. If it reads as advertising, the documentary register is broken.
8. **Hero doc that makes math the subject.** Per DBS — math is the TOOL, not the subject. The subject is how young people think.
