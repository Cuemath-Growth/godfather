# Meta Reel — format manual

The format manual for in-house Meta Reels (vertical video, 9:16, 15-30s for performance, up to 60s for brand). Distinct from Meta static (single frame) and from influencer Reels (creator-voice, different physics — see [[influencer-script.md]]).

KPI is CPTD via 3-second hold × full-completion × CTA click-through. Reel is the format where the parent's attention is most fragile but most rewarded — passive scroll, vertical orientation, vibe-first.

---

## When to invoke

You are writing a Meta Reel when ALL of these are true:
- Surface = Instagram Reels / Facebook Reels / 9:16 video on Meta
- Speaker = Cuemath (in-house brand voice) — NOT a creator
- Duration = 15-30s (performance) / 30-60s (brand) / up to 90s (hub-cut-down)
- Format = vertical video with on-screen text overlays, music or VO, locked end-card

If the speaker is a creator → [[influencer-script.md]].
If horizontal video on YouTube → [[youtube-video.md]].
If single static image → [[meta-static.md]].

---

## Physics of this surface

| Property | Reality |
|---|---|
| Read context | Parent scrolling Reels feed. Vertical orientation. Sound often off in first 3 seconds. |
| Time budget | **3-second hook decides everything.** Drop-off after 3s = creative dead. After 6s = body matters. After 12s = CTA matters. |
| Sound state | Default sound-off. Hook must work silent with captions. Sound-on parents are 30-40% but they show up after the 3s. |
| Vertical safe area | Top 15% + bottom 15% have UI overlay. Critical text in middle 70%. |
| Aspect | 9:16 (1080×1920) primary. 4:5 fallback for feed cross-post. |
| Caption rendering | On-screen captions required (Meta auto-captions are unreliable). Always burned-in. |
| Engagement loops | Watch-time × completion-rate × replay-rate drives algorithmic distribution. Hook + payoff structure matters. |

## The 3-beat shape — Reel-specific

CD v1 three-beat (Goal → Mechanism → Destination) applied to Reel:

| Beat | Time | Job | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Hook (Goal/Pain)** | 0-3s | 3-second hook — earns continued scroll | Visual + on-screen text. Sound usually off. Hook on the visual + caption. |
| **2 · Body (Mechanism)** | 3-12s | The proof, observed-moment, kid-behaviour or outcome | Tutor on screen / kid working / outcome reveal. Caption layered. |
| **3 · Destination (Close + CTA)** | 12-25s | Brand reveal + locked close card + CTA | Dark close-card frame · MathFit grounding · "Book a free 1:1 class" |

Per DBS rule: **first 5 seconds without Cuemath, without math, without "this is an ad."** Open on a human moment. The brand earns the reveal at the end.

For 15s performance Reels: tighten body to 3-10s, close at 10-15s.
For 30-60s brand Reels: body can breathe; brand reveal happens later.

## The 10 named parent-child dynamics (from Apr 25 DBS §8)

Every Reel picks ONE dynamic. Don't invent new ones. Use these as the body's central moment:

| Dynamic | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Surrender** | Parent tries to explain. Fails. Sits back. Defeated silence. |
| **The Nod** | Child pretends to understand. Parent believes it. Neither says anything. |
| **The Comparison** | "Priya got 98." Child's face. Nothing said back. |
| **The Late Night** | 11pm. Test tomorrow. Parent and child at the table. Exhausted. |
| **The Avoidance** | Child finds every reason not to sit down. Parent watches it happen. |
| **The Reversal** | Child corrects the parent. Parent doesn't know whether to be embarrassed or proud. |
| **The Question** | Child asks a math question nobody expected. Parent has no answer. |
| **The Report Card** | Parent opens it. Looks at the number. Looks at the child. Nothing is said. |
| **The Backpack** | Child sits at the table. New textbook unopened. Backpack still on. The face of someone who has decided not to try. |
| **The Morning** | Test day. Mother making breakfast, back turned. Daughter sits at the table. Notebook open from last night. |

Each dynamic is a 30-second short film — see DBS §8 scripts for the canonical examples.

## Tutor-on-camera Q&A subformat (calibrated 2026-05-13)

A distinct subformat for **in-house brand reels where the speaker is a Cuemath tutor**, not a parent and not a creator. Used when the brief says "tutor on camera, addressing the parent like a Q&A." Different physics from the parent-child-dynamic reels above.

### When to use
- Topic-led ads (Fractions / Word Problems / Decimals / Algebra etc.) where the parent is searching by topic + grade
- Audiences in research/evaluation mode who want the method explained, not a kid's transformation story
- Surfaces where tutor authority + product demonstration carry more weight than parent-observation

### The 3-beat shape — calibrated structure

| Beat | Time | What it does | What it must contain |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HOOK** | 0-5s | A specific parent quote the tutor heard, with two concrete examples | "A parent told me, 'My child knows what half means — she can point to half a pizza. But ask her for three-quarters of a chocolate bar, she's lost.'" Not a generic question card. Not a topic chyron. **A story the tutor is recounting.** |
| **MECHANISM** | 5-13s | Live dialogue inside a Cuemath lesson + named product features | "Here's what we do at Cuemath. On MathCanvas — our interactive whiteboard — I ask, 'Break this into 4 equal pieces.' She does. 'Now shade one. What fraction?' She thinks. 'One-quarter.'" **The parent watching is inside the lesson for 8 seconds.** Name MathCanvas + MathGym in voice + on-screen text. End the beat with the cue-don't-tell pedagogy made explicit: *"I'm there asking questions, not giving answers."* |
| **OUTCOME** | 13-17s | The parent's actual fear, resolved | "Now when she sees a fraction problem, she doesn't freeze." or "Now when he sees a word problem, he doesn't stare. He reaches for a pencil." **Name the fear (freeze / stare / panic / pause), not a vague "she's improved."** Then earn MathFit by what the body just showed: *"That's being MathFit — understanding built on understanding."* |
| **CLOSE** | 17-22s | End card | **Locked verbatim:** *"Same Cuemath Coach. Every Single Class. Making Your Child MathFit™."* + CTA *"Book a free 1:1 class"* |

### Topic-led opener constraint (Naina-locked May 13)

**First 3 words of the script = the topic name**, in natural tutor speech:
- ✅ *"Fractions is where I see most elementary kids get confused."* (Fractions = first word)
- ✅ *"Math word problems trip up most 4th graders."* (Math word problems = first 3 words)
- ✅ *"Decimals are where Grade 5 splits the room."* (Decimals = first word)
- ❌ *"Fractions in Grade 3."* — reads as a chyron, not natural speech
- ❌ *"Today I want to talk about fractions."* — topic comes too late

This overrides the DBS "first 5 seconds without Cuemath/math/ad" rule for this subformat only.

### Tone register

Conversational, not didactic. The tutor is talking to ONE parent, not lecturing a room:
- *"Here's what we do at Cuemath…"*
- *"But here's the key…"*
- *"That pause told me everything."*
- *"She's not memorising. She's discovering."*

If a line could appear in a B2B SaaS landing page, it's wrong. If it could appear in a tutor's WhatsApp reply to a parent, it's right.

### Product naming — MathCanvas + MathGym

Every tutor-Q&A reel should name **at least one** Cuemath product feature in the mechanism beat:
- **MathCanvas** — the interactive whiteboard. Use when the moment is collaborative (tutor + kid working together on screen, kid writing with digital pen, tutor seeing in real-time).
- **MathGym** — the self-paced practice / puzzle layer. Use when the moment is independent practice / pattern repetition / spaced practice.

Both can appear in the same reel — MathCanvas for the teach moment, MathGym for the practice moment. Cycle on-screen text accordingly.

### Anchor: the canonical Grade 3 Fractions script (Naina-authored, May 13)

> [0-5s] HOOK
> TUTOR: *"Fractions is where I see most elementary school kids get confused. A parent came to me and said, 'My child knows what half means. She can point to half a pizza. But if I ask her, "Show me three-quarters of this chocolate bar," she's lost. That's because she has memorised the word but doesn't actually see it.'"*
>
> [5-13s] MECHANISM
> TUTOR: *"Here's what we do at Cuemath. On our MathCanvas platform — that's our interactive whiteboard — I put a visual in front of her. A circle divided into 4 equal parts. I ask, 'Break this into 4 equal pieces. Shade one. What fraction did you shade?' She uses her pen directly on screen. I see exactly what she's doing in real time. She says, 'One-quarter.' Now I ask, 'If you shade 2 pieces, what do you have?' She thinks. She shades. She answers.*
>
> *But here's the key: she's not memorising. She's discovering. And I'm there, asking questions, not giving answers."*
>
> [13-17s] OUTCOME
> TUTOR: *"Now when she sees a problem with fractions, she doesn't freeze. That's being MathFit — understanding built on understanding, not memorization."*

Read this aloud before drafting any tutor-Q&A reel. It is the register.

**Note on the close card in this anchor:** Naina's May 13 draft of this script ended with *"Same Dedicated Coach. Stays Long term. Making Your Child MathFit."* — that was a one-off. Production close card is the May 10 lock: *"Same Cuemath Coach. Every Single Class. Making Your Child MathFit™."* Confirmed May 13.

### Subformat-specific failure modes (additional to the universal list below)

1. **Topic-card opener.** *"Fractions in Grade 3."* reads as a chyron/section header. Real tutors don't speak in section headers. Open with a sentence — *"Fractions is where I see most kids get confused"* — that happens to lead with the topic.
2. **Generic parent question on a card.** *"Why doesn't she get fractions?"* is a tagline. Replace with the parent's actual words, with two concrete examples, voiced by the tutor recounting the conversation.
3. **Narrating the lesson instead of showing it.** *"Tuesday she draws fractions. Wednesday she explains."* is abstract. Put the parent INSIDE the lesson: tutor asks → kid responds → tutor asks again.
4. **No product name.** Every tutor-Q&A reel must name MathCanvas or MathGym (or both). Otherwise the brand equity opportunity is wasted.
5. **No OUTCOME beat.** Going straight from mechanism to close card skips the payoff. The parent's fear named in HOOK must be resolved in OUTCOME, in 4-5s, with a specific verb (freeze / stare / pause / panic).
6. **Cue-don't-tell paraphrased instead of named.** *"We do it the other way around"* is vague. *"I'm there asking questions, not giving answers"* is the moat made visible. Don't paraphrase.
7. **Tutor named in VO.** The tutor is on camera — her face is the proof. Don't have her say her name. *"Hi, I'm Anita Ma'am"* introductions are off-brand for this format.
8. **MathFit defined too early.** The term lands in the OUTCOME beat after the lesson body has earned it. Saying *"that's MathFit"* in the hook is unearned.

## Lock / Free / Anchor per funnel stage

### TOFU Reels (cold awareness — DBS Hub films + cut-downs)

**Locked:**
- First 5 seconds without Cuemath / without math / without "this is an ad"
- One named dynamic from DBS §8 (don't invent new)
- Three-beat shape (Hook → Body → Brand-reveal close)
- Locked close card verbatim
- CTA: "Book a free 1:1 class" (HS US exception)
- Coach visible by tenure or memory (not bare-name as outcome source)
- Single MathFit dimension (or none) — never two

**Free:**
- Which dynamic from the 10
- Cast (parent gender, kid age, ethnicity per market)
- Setting (kitchen, bedroom, kid's desk, car, school gate, dinner table)
- Sound design (silent + captions / VO / score / room tone)
- Tutor visibility (named, anonymous, behind-screen, on-camera)

**Anchor:**
- DBS §8 hub film scripts (canonical examples)
- Per-market voice canon (which dynamic lands for which audience)
- Trustpilot CSV slice for verbatim parent VO lines

### MOFU Reels (warmed audience comparison / consideration)

**Locked:** All TOFU locks. Plus: comparison framing one-sided (never names competitors).

**Free:**
- Comparison structure (side-by-side · before/after · documentary-style follow)
- Testimonial-style VO (parent reading verbatim Trustpilot quote on camera)
- Method-walkthrough scenes (tutor with kid working through a problem)

### BOFU Reels (intent-hot conversion / retargeting)

**Locked:** All TOFU locks. Plus: no offers / no urgency / no discounts in close.

**Free:**
- Direct trial-walkthrough format
- Number-led outcome reveal at the open (counter-pattern — earns the 3s differently)
- Specific testimonial spotlight (named parent, named outcome)

## Variation axes — combinatorial differentiation

When shipping N Reel variants:

1. **Opening visual** — kid's face · artifact (textbook / report card) · parent gesture · setting establish · close-up on object · silence shot
2. **Dynamic chosen** — one of the 10 (don't repeat across variants in the same campaign)
3. **Voice/POV** — silent + captions · parent VO · kid VO · documentary-narrator · tutor-on-camera direct
4. **Tutor visibility** — on-camera face · behind-screen with name caption · voice-only · tenure-card overlay
5. **Closing tempo** — silence → close card · music swell → close card · kid line → close card · parent reflection → close card

If two Reel variants share 3+ axes, that's pattern lock. Re-vary.

## CPTD gates

1. **3-second hold rate** — must exceed cell-weighted benchmark from Godfather
2. **Completion rate** — full-watch ≥ 25% target
3. **Click-through to LP** — varies by funnel stage
4. **Sound-off comprehensibility** — verify captions carry the message without audio

---

## Failure modes — Reel-specific

1. **First 3 seconds is brand reveal.** "Cuemath" appears in first frame. DBS rule violated. Drop-off spikes.
2. **Sound-on dependency.** Hook only works with audio. 60% of viewers see it sound-off and skip.
3. **Captions burned-in too small / wrong placement.** Top 15% / bottom 15% of vertical safe area covered by UI. Caption gets clipped.
4. **Invented dynamic.** Trying to be original on the body when the 10 named DBS dynamics already cover the space.
5. **Multi-dynamic confusion.** Trying to land The Reversal AND The Nod AND The Comparison in one Reel. Pick one.
6. **Locked close card improvised.** Trying to "make it fit better" — close card is verbatim. Don't.
7. **Parent VO names the framework.** "She's now fluent, understanding, applying, reasoning" — FUAR/MathFit framework names spoken in VO. Banned per [[feedback_frameworks_not_in_vo]]. Use outcome language: "she explains her steps before she solves."
8. **Cross-market borrowing.** AU Reel uses "Grade 5" or US Reel uses "Year 5." Wrong-market vocabulary.
9. **Bare-name coach in body.** "Coach Karthik delivered" — marketplace framing. Use tenure / memory.
10. **Reel longer than 30s for performance, shorter than 30s for brand.** Wrong duration for the job. Performance = 15-25s; brand = 45-60s+.
